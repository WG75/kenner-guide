Debut Kenner Cardback reference
Source: Variant Villain reference workbook, Figure -> Cardback Matrix and Cardback Summary legend. Cardback research date 14 September 2026 (Method & Scope).
Debut Kenner Cardback means the workbook column Earliest / exact cardback. It is not a confirmed debut for most figures, and it is not Warren's own Debut Card Back list. Do not silently overwrite one with the other.
Debut Kenner Cardback, compatible cardbacks (Other documented cardbacks / range) and factory matching are different. A figure on a card does not prove every variant belongs with that card. Factory matching is not established in this workbook (no card-manufacturing source or run). A rationale line below is repeated only when the workbook states that factory-to-card link, with its evidence label.
Evidence: Exact / explicitly documented -> documented. Confirmed production range -> documented (range only, not an exact card). Regional family / broad match -> probable. Unconfirmed exact micro-variant -> unknown.
Warren's valid Kenner families: 12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92. 48-back is in the Cardback Summary (ESB 48A1, 48A2, 48B1, 48B2, 48C; ROTJ 48A1, 48A2, 48A3) but is not on that list. 14, 17, 18, 30, 37, 50 and 70 are not on it either. Flagged where they appear.
96 figure pages. Variant lines are only Exact or Confirmed production range. Aliases are only names already written in the figure title. Packaging is the workbook Era field.

Figure Name: Ben (Obi-Wan) Kenobi
Aliases: Ben (Obi-Wan) Kenobi; Obi-Wan
Packaging: Star Wars
Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. No figure-level family range is stated. Variant lines below are the workbook's Earliest / exact cardback values, not a single debut for every variant.
Factory matching: not established in this workbook.
Variant lines (Exact or Confirmed production range only):
- Unitoy white-hair and grey-hair combinations with differing eye/hand paint. Factory / origin: Unitoy. Earliest / exact cardback: Star Wars 12-back. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: SW 12-back onward; saber mould transition documented around 12C. Rationale: Early Unitoy/Kader Ben production is documented in the 12-back era; exact hair shade is not assigned to a single sub-back. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/sw-cardbacks/.
- Kader white hair/pale-brown eyes; Kader white hair/black eyes; Kader 'Lincoln beard'; Kader grey-hair shades. Factory / origin: Kader. Earliest / exact cardback: Star Wars 12-back. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: SW 12-back onward; saber mould transition documented around 12C. Rationale: Early Unitoy/Kader Ben production is documented in the 12-back era; exact hair shade is not assigned to a single sub-back. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/sw-cardbacks/.
- Kader No COO yellowish/blueish-grey hair; Kader No COO white-hair dull-black collar; Kader No COO white-hair glossy-black collar. Factory / origin: Kader. Earliest / exact cardback: Late Kenner / Trilogo. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: Trilogo cards documented for late Kader No-COO Ben. Rationale: Late Kader No-COO Ben variants are documented on Trilogo cards. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/trilogo-cardback-guide/.
