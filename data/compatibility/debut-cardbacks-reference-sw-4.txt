Debut Kenner Cardback reference
Source: Variant Villain reference workbook, Figure -> Cardback Matrix and Cardback Summary legend. Cardback research date 14 September 2026 (Method & Scope).
Debut Kenner Cardback means the workbook column Earliest / exact cardback. It is not a confirmed debut for most figures, and it is not Warren's own Debut Card Back list. Do not silently overwrite one with the other.
Debut Kenner Cardback, compatible cardbacks (Other documented cardbacks / range) and factory matching are different. A figure on a card does not prove every variant belongs with that card. Factory matching is not established in this workbook (no card-manufacturing source or run). A rationale line below is repeated only when the workbook states that factory-to-card link, with its evidence label.
Evidence: Exact / explicitly documented -> documented. Confirmed production range -> documented (range only, not an exact card). Regional family / broad match -> probable. Unconfirmed exact micro-variant -> unknown.
Warren's valid Kenner families: 12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92. 48-back is in the Cardback Summary (ESB 48A1, 48A2, 48B1, 48B2, 48C; ROTJ 48A1, 48A2, 48A3) but is not on that list. 14, 17, 18, 30, 37, 50 and 70 are not on it either. Flagged where they appear.
96 figure pages. Variant lines are only Exact or Confirmed production range. Aliases are only names already written in the figure title. Packaging is the workbook Era field.
Figure Name: Jawa
Aliases: Jawa
Packaging: Star Wars
Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. Workbook figure-level family range (not a confirmed debut): Star Wars 12-back; many continued onto SW 20/21 and later cards.
Factory matching: not established in this workbook.
Variant lines (Exact or Confirmed production range only):
- Kader HK vinyl-cape rectangular very-dark-brown bandolier/transparent oval eyes. Factory / origin: Kader. Earliest / exact cardback: Star Wars 12A. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: SW 12A–12C vinyl-cape era. Rationale: Kader vinyl-cape Jawa belongs to the early 12-back VC run; exact paint/bandolier micro-variant is not forced to one sub-back. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/jawa-cardbacks/.
- Toltoys vinyl cape rounded bandolier. Factory / origin: Toltoys. Earliest / exact cardback: ESB 41E. Evidence: documented. Other documented cardbacks / range: ESB 41E Toltoys. Rationale: The mixed Toltoys vinyl-cape variant is traced by Variant Villain to an ESB 41E cardback. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/jawa-cardbacks/.
- Unitoy F3 rectangular bandolier/small-dot eyes; Unitoy F4 rounded bandolier with small-dot or almond eyes. Factory / origin: Unitoy. Earliest / exact cardback: Star Wars 12-back. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: SW 12-back through ESB 47-back documented. Rationale: Unitoy cloth-cape Jawa debuted in the SW 12-back period and is documented through ESB 47-back. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/jawa-cardbacks/.
- Lili Ledy rounded grey-brown/purple-brown/dark-brown bandoliers; Lili Ledy removable hood light stitching; Lili Ledy removable hood dark stitching; Lili Ledy attached hood. Factory / origin: Lili Ledy. Earliest / exact cardback: Lili Ledy 50-back Regreso [50-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review]. Evidence: documented. Other documented cardbacks / range: 50-back Regreso [50-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review]. Rationale: Lili Ledy Jawa is documented on the 50-back Regreso card [50-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review]. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/lili-ledy-cardbacks/.
