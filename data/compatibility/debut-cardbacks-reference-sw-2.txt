Debut Kenner Cardback reference
Source: Variant Villain reference workbook, Figure -> Cardback Matrix and Cardback Summary legend. Cardback research date 14 September 2026 (Method & Scope).
Debut Kenner Cardback means the workbook column Earliest / exact cardback. It is not a confirmed debut for most figures, and it is not Warren's own Debut Card Back list. Do not silently overwrite one with the other.
Debut Kenner Cardback, compatible cardbacks (Other documented cardbacks / range) and factory matching are different. A figure on a card does not prove every variant belongs with that card. Factory matching is not established in this workbook (no card-manufacturing source or run). A rationale line below is repeated only when the workbook states that factory-to-card link, with its evidence label.
Evidence: Exact / explicitly documented -> documented. Confirmed production range -> documented (range only, not an exact card). Regional family / broad match -> probable. Unconfirmed exact micro-variant -> unknown.
Warren's valid Kenner families: 12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92. 48-back is in the Cardback Summary (ESB 48A1, 48A2, 48B1, 48B2, 48C; ROTJ 48A1, 48A2, 48A3) but is not on that list. 14, 17, 18, 30, 37, 50 and 70 are not on it either. Flagged where they appear.
96 figure pages. Variant lines are only Exact or Confirmed production range. Aliases are only names already written in the figure title. Packaging is the workbook Era field.
Figure Name: R2-D2 (Solid Dome)
Aliases: R2-D2 (Solid Dome); Solid Dome
Packaging: Star Wars
Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. Workbook figure-level family range (not a confirmed debut): Star Wars 12-back; many continued onto SW 20/21 and later cards.
Factory matching: not established in this workbook.
Variant lines (Exact or Confirmed production range only):
- Unitoy very-dark-blue dome/halftone Early Bird-era sticker. Factory / origin: Unitoy. Earliest / exact cardback: Early Bird / Star Wars 12-back. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: Early Star Wars 12-back. Rationale: This very-dark-blue Unitoy version is identified as Early Bird-era / earliest carded production. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/sw-cardbacks/.
- Smile mid-blue SW-era. Factory / origin: Smile. Earliest / exact cardback: Star Wars 12-back. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: Star Wars 12/20/21 era. Rationale: The checklist itself identifies this Smile paint version as SW-era; exact sub-back is not assigned. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/sw-cardbacks/.
- Smile mid-blue ESB-era. Factory / origin: Smile. Earliest / exact cardback: ESB-era card. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: ESB production. Rationale: The checklist identifies this Smile version as ESB-era; exact ESB sub-back is not independently proven. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/sw-cardbacks/.
- Lili Ledy Regreso vibrant dark-blue; Lili Ledy Regreso dull dark-blue; Lili Ledy Regreso light-blue; Lili Ledy Regreso mid-blue/PBP-associated. Factory / origin: Lili Ledy. Earliest / exact cardback: Lili Ledy Regreso. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: Regreso domestic Mexican card family. Rationale: This is explicitly identified as a Lili Ledy Regreso production variant. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/lili-ledy-cardbacks/.
- Lili Ledy Retorno 1978 No COO vibrant dark-blue. Factory / origin: Lili Ledy. Earliest / exact cardback: Lili Ledy Retorno. Evidence: documented. Other documented cardbacks / range: Retorno domestic Mexican card family. Rationale: The variant is specifically identified as a Lili Ledy Retorno 1978 No-COO version. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/lili-ledy-cardbacks/.
- F5 Unitoy mid-blue dull dome/small red eye; F5 Unitoy light-blue-green dome/larger crimson eye. Factory / origin: Unitoy. Earliest / exact cardback: ESB 41-back. Evidence: documented. Other documented cardbacks / range: Mid-ESB 41-back documented. Rationale: Variant Villain notes the F5 Unitoy solid-dome figure is so far documented on mid-ESB 41-back cards. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/sw-cardbacks/.
- Taiwan dark-blue; Taiwan mid-blue; Taiwan green-blue. Factory / origin: Taiwan / Universal Manufacturers. Earliest / exact cardback: Star Wars 12-back. Evidence: documented. Other documented cardbacks / range: Star Wars 12-back only. Rationale: Variant Villain states the Taiwan solid-dome R2-D2 was a 12-back-only figure.
