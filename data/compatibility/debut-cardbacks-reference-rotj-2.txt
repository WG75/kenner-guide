Debut Kenner Cardback reference
Source: Variant Villain reference workbook, Figure -> Cardback Matrix and Cardback Summary legend. Cardback research date 14 September 2026 (Method & Scope).
Debut Kenner Cardback means the workbook column Earliest / exact cardback. It is not a confirmed debut for most figures, and it is not Warren's own Debut Card Back list. Do not silently overwrite one with the other.
Debut Kenner Cardback, compatible cardbacks (Other documented cardbacks / range) and factory matching are different. A figure on a card does not prove every variant belongs with that card. Factory matching is not established in this workbook (no card-manufacturing source or run). A rationale line below is repeated only when the workbook states that factory-to-card link, with its evidence label.
Evidence: Exact / explicitly documented -> documented. Confirmed production range -> documented (range only, not an exact card). Regional family / broad match -> probable. Unconfirmed exact micro-variant -> unknown.
Warren's valid Kenner families: 12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92. 48-back is in the Cardback Summary (ESB 48A1, 48A2, 48B1, 48B2, 48C; ROTJ 48A1, 48A2, 48A3) but is not on that list. 14, 17, 18, 30, 37, 50 and 70 are not on it either. Flagged where they appear.
96 figure pages. Variant lines are only Exact or Confirmed production range. Aliases are only names already written in the figure title. Packaging is the workbook Era field.

Figure Name: Klaatu
Aliases: Klaatu
Packaging: Return of the Jedi
Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. No figure-level family range is stated. Variant lines below are the workbook's Earliest / exact cardback values, not a single debut for every variant.
Factory matching: not established in this workbook.
Variant lines (Exact or Confirmed production range only):
- Green painted face/cream fluffy skirt; green painted face/grey-brown flat skirt. Factory / origin: Smile. Earliest / exact cardback: Kenner ROTJ 65. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: Kenner 65 / 77 / 79; UK Palitoy also documented. Rationale: Smile Klaatu is documented on Kenner 65, 77 and 79 cardbacks. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/rotj-cardbacks/.
- blue-green moulded face/mid-brown vest/cream fluffy skirt; blue-grey moulded face/mid-brown vest/cream fluffy skirt; blue-grey moulded face/dark-brown vest/tan-leg mix/flat skirt; green-blue moulded face/mid-brown vest/flat skirt. Factory / origin: Unitoy. Earliest / exact cardback: Kenner ROTJ 65. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: Kenner 65 / 77. Rationale: Unitoy Klaatu is documented on 65 and 77; the fluffy-skirt phase appears earlier. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/rotj-cardbacks/.
- dark-green painted face/dark-brown shoulders/fluffy skirt/leather belt; mid-green face/dark-brown shoulders/fluffy skirt/fabric belt; dark-green face/mid-brown shoulders/flat skirt/leather belt; mid-green face/mid-brown shoulders/flat skirt/fabric belt. Factory / origin: Lili Ledy. Earliest / exact cardback: Lili Ledy 14-back Retorno [14-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review]. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: 14-back Retorno; 50-back Regreso; Kenner 65 Made in Mexico [14-back, 50-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review]. Rationale: Lili Ledy Klaatu is documented across these Mexican/MIM card families. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/lili-ledy-cardbacks/.
Figure source: https://www.variantvillain.com/characters/rotj/klaatu/
