Debut Kenner Cardback reference
Source: Variant Villain reference workbook, Figure -> Cardback Matrix and Cardback Summary legend. Cardback research date 14 September 2026 (Method & Scope).
Debut Kenner Cardback means the workbook column Earliest / exact cardback. It is not a confirmed debut for most figures, and it is not Warren's own Debut Card Back list. Do not silently overwrite one with the other.
Debut Kenner Cardback, compatible cardbacks (Other documented cardbacks / range) and factory matching are different. A figure on a card does not prove every variant belongs with that card. Factory matching is not established in this workbook (no card-manufacturing source or run). A rationale line below is repeated only when the workbook states that factory-to-card link, with its evidence label.
Evidence: Exact / explicitly documented -> documented. Confirmed production range -> documented (range only, not an exact card). Regional family / broad match -> probable. Unconfirmed exact micro-variant -> unknown.
Warren's valid Kenner families: 12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92. 48-back is in the Cardback Summary (ESB 48A1, 48A2, 48B1, 48B2, 48C; ROTJ 48A1, 48A2, 48A3) but is not on that list. 14, 17, 18, 30, 37, 50 and 70 are not on it either. Flagged where they appear.
96 figure pages. Variant lines are only Exact or Confirmed production range. Aliases are only names already written in the figure title. Packaging is the workbook Era field.

- Smile HK pinkish face/snap cloak/blue saber; Smile HK pinkish face/stitched cloak/blue saber; Smile HK stitched cloak/blue-green saber; Smile HK pale face/green saber; Smile No COO tanned face/light-green saber. Factory / origin: Smile. Earliest / exact cardback: ROTJ 65-back. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: ROTJ 65-back through late Trilogo. Rationale: Smile Luke Jedi debuted in the 65-back era and continues through late Trilogo production. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/luke-jedi-cardbacks/.
- Taiwan dark-brown moulded head/snap cloak; Taiwan light-brown moulded head/snap cloak; Taiwan painted face/stitched cloak. Factory / origin: Taiwan / Universal Manufacturers. Earliest / exact cardback: ROTJ-era. Evidence: documented (range only, not an exact card). Other documented cardbacks / range: ROTJ / Trilogo carding documented. Rationale: Taiwan Luke Jedi examples are documented across ROTJ/Trilogo carding; exact head/cloak micro-variant requires sealed evidence. Cardback source: https://www.thevariantvillains.com/cardbacks/luke-jedi-cardbacks/.
- Top Toys green cloak/brown stitching; Top Toys brown cloak/green stitching; Top Toys green cloak/brown stitching/vertical ribbing. Factory / origin: Top Toys. Earliest / exact cardback: Top Toys 17A [17-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review]. Evidence: documented. Other documented cardbacks / range: Top Toys 17A / 17B [17-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review]. Rationale: Top Toys Luke Jedi is documented on 17A and 17B cards [17-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review].
