Top Toys Darth Vader
Source name: Variant Villain. Source URL: https://www.variantvillain.com/variations/top-toys/top-toys-darth-vader/ Reliability: high. Recorded: 2026-10-04.
- Top Toys Guide
- Top Toys Darth Vader
- DIFFERENCES: Compared to his Kenner counterpart, the Top Toys Darth Vader is
- Moulded in a harder, more brittle plastic
- Shorter in height
- Has excessive flashing, typically on limbs and helmet
- Has lack of ‘sculpt' detail, see chest plate & mouth piece on the helmet for good examples
- Has EPM marks on all limbs
- Has no peg holes in the underside of the feet
- Has a different spray mask on chest plate
- Has paler red paint details
- Was packaged with a semi-transparent v6 Lightsaber & unique cape
- Has no COO markings
- Top Toys on the right
- Top Toys centre
- Poor QC also resulted in minor differences between batches.
- Poorly positioned lightsaber hole
- The Top Toys Darth Vader came with a semi-transparent v6 Lightsaber and is compared to the Kenner Lightsaber.
- DIFFERENCES: Compared to the Kenner lightsaber, the Top Toys lightsaber is
- Lighter in colour
- Has a thicker Tip
- Has a shorter main body
- Is semi-transparent with flecking
- Has a large EPM on the side (*)
- Floats during the water test
- Tops Toys Lightsaber on top / Kenner bottom
- Tops Toys Lightsaber x2
- WATER TEST
- Both lightsabers float when placed in water
- Kenner lightsaber sinks when tapped
- Top Toys/Kenner Lightsaber water test
- The Top Toys Darth Vader came with a completely unique cape compared to any other Darth Vader figure.
- DIFFERENCES: Compared to the Kenner cape, the Top Toys cape has
- Squared off bottom corners
- A more pronounced peak
- A unique “leather” effect finish to both inner and outer sides

Top Toys Chief Chirpa
Source name: Variant Villain. Source URL: https://www.variantvillain.com/variations/top-toys/top-toys-chief-chirpa/ Reliability: high. Recorded: 2026-10-04.
- TOP TOYS GUIDE
- Top Toys Chief Chirpa
- Compared to his Kenner counterpart, the Top Toys Chief Chirpa is
- Moulded in a harder, more brittle plastic
- Shorter in height
- Slimmer in width
- Has excessive flashing, typically on hands and legs
- Lacking a lot of ‘sculpt' detail, the ewoks fur is the easiest and most obvious example
- Has EPM marks on all limbs
- Has no peg holes in the underside of the feet
- Has a glossy black belt/eyes
- Has an semi-transparent white medallion
- Was packaged with a darker brown v5 staff & unique hood
- Has no COO markings
- Top Toys on the right
- Top Toys centre
- Kenner on the left,Top Toys in the middle and on the right
- Top Toys on the left and right
- Top Toys in the centre
- Poor QC also resulted in minor differences between batches.
- On this same figure we can see over-spray around the eyes and belt as the spray mask wasn't pressing down correctly during this process.
- Poor mouldings, the example above shows a large gap between the torso halves.
- The Top Toys Chief Chirpa came with a dark brown v5 staff and is compared to the Kenner accessory.
- DIFFERENCES: Compared to the Kenner staff, the Top Toys staff is
- Darker in colour
- Shorter in length
- Lacks most of the ‘sculpt' detail
- Has a three large EPM on the side
- Floats during the water test
- Tops Toys staff on top / Kenner bottom
- WATER TEST
- Both staffs float when placed or dropped in water
- The Top Toys Chief Chirpa came with a dark brown hood and is compared to the Kenner accessory.
- DIFFERENCES
- Compared to the Kenner hood, the Top Toys hood is
- Smaller in size, note the hood often covers the lower section of the month (see photo above)
- Rough edges with lots of flashing
- Has a large EPM inside the hood*
- * Depending on the figure used some Kenner hoods also feature an EPM
