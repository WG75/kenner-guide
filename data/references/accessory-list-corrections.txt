Topic: Accessory list corrections
Source: owner correction, authoritative. Where a later conflict is not one of these corrections, variantvillain.com stays the authority and the stored page text is kept.

Where the false pieces came from:
The photo completeness list is tools/build-kenner-debut-figures.py, copied into data/kenner-debut-figures.json. It is not a Wikipedia accessory column. The Wikipedia Kenner table columns are wave, debut package card, action figure, product number and debut year. That page does not name accessories.

Removed from the completeness list and the gap report:
- Han Solo (Bespin Outfit) has no cape. His blaster stays.
- Romba has no bow. His hood and spear stay on the character page and are not on the completeness list.
- Paploo has no spear. His hood, quiver and staff stay on the character page and are not on the completeness list.
- Anakin Skywalker was never packaged with an accessory.
- R2-D2 (Sensorscope). The sensorscope is built into the figure, not a loose accessory. The figure notes still describe the extending sensorscope.
- Lando Calrissian (Skiff Guard Disguise) has no cape. The list is the Lando Skiff Helmet and the Vibro Axe.
- Teebo has no lightsaber. The round-tip saber sentence, and the lettered-lightsaber sentence copied with it, were removed from his figure file. His list stays hood, axe and horn.

Renamed to the owner's word or the Variant Villain heading:
- 4-LOM blaster is the 4-LOM Rifle. The page also heads 4-LOM Cloak, so the cloak is on the list.
- Zuckuss blaster is the Zuckuss Rifle.
- Rancor Keeper axe is a staff. The page also heads the hood, so the hood is on the list.
- Barada polearm is the skiff vibro staff.
- Weequay pike is the Vibro Axe.
- Nikto's weapon is the Skiff Vibro Staff, the same piece as Barada and Yak Face. Owner-verified, 11 Oct 2026. This overrides the Variant Villain heading, Skiff Vibro Axe. The staff guide has exactly two variants: Smile M1 Skiff Vibro Staff and Lili Ledy M2 Skiff Vibro Staff. Barada and Yak Face are Smile-only, so they came with M1. Nikto came with Smile M1 or Lili Ledy M2. Imperial Gunnery notes the Lili Ledy staff is a darker grey than the Kenner/Smile one.
- Hammerhead's empty list is the Imperial Blaster.
- Rebel Soldier rifle is the Bespin Blaster.
- Rebel Commander blaster is the Hoth Rebel Rifle.
- IG-88 also has an Imperial Blaster. The rifle is named IG-88 Rifle.
- Klaatu also has a Vibro Axe. The skirt stays.
- Klaatu (in Skiff Guard Outfit) is the skiff vibro staff and the Vibro Axe. The skirt was the other Klaatu's piece. The painted hood on that page is part of the figure, not a loose accessory.
- Squid Head rifle is the Bespin Blaster. The skirt and belt stay, and the cape heading is now on the list.
- Emperor's Royal Guard pike is listed as a staff, which is the page heading. That heading links to the Emperor's Royal Guard Pike guide, so it is not a gap.
- Luke Skywalker (Bespin Fatigues) is the Rebel Blaster and the handheld lightsaber.
- Luke Skywalker (Jedi Knight Outfit) is the handheld lightsaber, the cloak and the Palace Blaster.
- Luke Skywalker (X-Wing Pilot) is the Rebel Blaster. That page's accessory heading is the Rebel Blaster guide, not a lightsaber.
- Luke Skywalker (in Battle Poncho) is the endor helmet, poncho, belt and Palace Blaster.
- Luke Skywalker (Imperial Stormtrooper Outfit) is the Imperial Blaster and the helmet.
- Princess Leia Organa (in Combat Poncho) is the Leia Endor helmet, Endor blaster, Leia blaster and Leia Endor belt.

- At-At Commander blaster is the Bespin Blaster.
- (Twin-Pod) Cloud Car Pilot is the Pilot Blaster and the commlink. The commlink is the Commlink Guide.

- C-3PO (Removable Limbs) has the net.
