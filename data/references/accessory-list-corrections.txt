Topic: Accessory list corrections
Source: owner correction, authoritative. Where a later conflict is not one of the three corrections below, variantvillain.com stays the authority and the stored page text is kept.

Where the three false pieces came from:
The photo completeness list is tools/build-kenner-debut-figures.py, copied into data/kenner-debut-figures.json. It is not a Wikipedia accessory column. The Wikipedia Kenner table columns are wave, debut package card, action figure, product number and debut year. That page does not name accessories. The cape, bow and spear were typed into the completeness list by hand.

Removed from the completeness list, the "He looks complete, with his..." line, and the gap report:
- Han Solo (Bespin Outfit) has no cape. His Variant Villain page names a Bespin Blaster and no cape. The blaster stays.
- Romba has no bow. His Variant Villain page says he was packed with his own hood and a spear shared with Wicket. Those two are not added to the completeness list here.
- Paploo has no spear. His page headings are a staff and a hood, and one sentence on that page says hood, quiver and spear. The owner correction is that he has no spear. The guide does not give him one. The hood, quiver and staff named on the page are not added to the completeness list here.
- Anakin Skywalker had a lightsaber on the completeness list. His Variant Villain page says he was never packaged with an accessory, so that lightsaber was removed. This is the same kind of error, and the page states it directly.
