- This guide is dedicated to the mould-colour variants produced by Kenner in the Asian factories (Taiwan, Macau, Smile, Unitoy and Kader).
- Changes to mould-colour could occur during production or simply because different factories were involved.
- It is worth noting that most vintage Star Wars figures have limbs made from two different colours (one painted, one moulded).
- The factory then had to choose: Do I mould the arms in “colour A” and paint it with “colour B” or the other way around?
- This is the 2021 reworked edition of this guide. I hope you enjoy it.
- If you want to spot a “white moulded legs” Han Solo take a look at the peg holes and look for tiny rubs to the feet or to the hip.
- Is it better to risk paint rubs to the feet (playwear) or to the hips (from moving the legs)?

- Short summary on General Madine
- UNITOY figures come with flesh or grey moulded head and with grey moulded arms and legs.
- SMILE figures come with flesh moulded head and grey moulded arms and legs.
- TAIWAN figures come with flesh moulded head and black moulded arms and legs.
