- 65 figures are shown on the standard black & yellow background, Logray and Chief Chirpa Ewoks are blacked out.
- Identical to 65A-ROTJ except the Ewok figures are visible.
- Figures group photo identical to 65B. The “Free! The Emperor” offer is printed on lower 1/3rd of the card.

- Identical to 65C-ROTJ with a large “Free! Anakin Skywalker” offer sticker on lower 1/3rd of the card.

- RETURN OF THE JEDI: 79 Back
- Identical to 79A-ROTJ with a large “Free! Anakin Skywalker” offer sticker on lower 1/3rd of the card.
- Identical to 79B-ROTJ except the Anakin Skywalker offer is printed.

- The Trilogo line was introduced with the second wave of “Return of the Jedi” figures.
- It was the culminating multi-lingual packaging standard set by Palitoy in 1984 in order to globalize packaging and marketing efforts for the entire European market.
- Widely available, they ended-up being sold by packs or at clearance circa 1986, and were even retailed in the U.S.A.

- Trilogo front released for the latest 15 new figures from the Kenner POTF series. It now features a barcode.
- TRILOGO EUROPEAN LICENSEE (alphabetical)
- Trilogo Harbert cards are always from Palitoy Trilogos, with an additional Italian safety regulatory sticker attached on the back.
- They are recognizable on the front from the larger hanger tab and very large blister.
