- ROTJ/TL 70A CARDBACK
- The 70A card variation is a transition between the ROTJ 65-back cards and the Tri-Logo 70-back cards.
- The front is similar to the 65A, 65B and 65C variations with the text Ages 4 and up in multiple languages on the top left and right hand
- The ROTJ logo has no black border underneath it though, making it the same as the 45C and 65D variations in that respect.
