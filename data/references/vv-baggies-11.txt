- Nien Nunb Find
- Here is what the guy wrote about what he knows about his great find.
- When I entered the scene I believe in September 1977, it was still very busy, selling out both shows on Friday and Saturday nights, and Sunday matinees.
- I don't know, but it's possible they ran newspaper ads with a coupon, and something like the first 50 customers each show got a toy.
- That kind of promotion was not unusual in those days, but I have no proof. Maybe with a little more research.
- Today is the first time I've ever emptied the box to see what was really in there, and must say I was a bit disappointed.
- It's possible each figure came in bulk in it's own box, and the theatre just lumped them all into one at the end of the promotion.
