This file is the durable record for vintage Kenner R2-D2 solid dome (1977, 38200). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

## Where the stamp is

R2's licensing line and country stamp are on the underside of the body, around the base hole, not on the legs. Do not tell a collector to look on the legs for this figure.

## Stamp layout

Unitoy M1, Smile M2 and Unitoy M5 each read '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977 MADE IN HONG KONG' as one continuous line along the top. They are hard to separate without the guide beside the figure. The red marks on COO guide 3.7 show the line spacing between Unitoy and Smile.

Taiwan M6 reads '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977 MADE IN TAIWAN' as one continuous line. The red circle and arrows on the guide mark that line.

Kader M3, Kader M4 and Kader M7 have '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' at the top and 'HONG KONG' at the bottom, on the opposite side. Lili Ledy Regreso uses that same Kader layout. Lili Ledy Retorno is No COO, and the licensing line shows 1978.

Warren also mentioned a Kader M5. Family 5 is Unitoy. Keep that assignment. Do not add a Kader M5.

## Cavity numbers

From the guide image. Kader F3 cavity 1 or 2, and the number is not visible from the other side. Kader F4 cavity 5 or 6. Lili Ledy Retorno also cavity 5 or 6. Kader F7 cavity 3 or 4.

## Families

- Unitoy M1 (VV F1). Very dark blue dome with a 2-part halftone sticker and mid-blue legs, then a 1-part sticker, then a mid-blue dome. Poch is built from Unitoy or Kader parts.
- Smile M2 (VV F2). Large crimson eye and a darker wedge.
- Kader M3 (VV F3). Cavity 1 or 2. Dark blue dome, then mid-blue. Matt mid-blue legs. Large crimson-red eye.
- Lili Ledy Regreso (VV F3). Same stamp as Kader. Light-blue legs. The guide notes a hard or soft torso on both Lili Ledy COOs.
- PBP. A Lili Ledy Regreso figure carded in Spain, with a B-type sticker. The PBP note does not belong on Lili Ledy Retorno.
- Kader M4 (VV F4). Cavity 5 or 6. A short run and a minor COO variation. The torso mould later went to R5-D4.
- Lili Ledy Retorno (VV F4, app M4). No COO. Licensing shows 1978. Cavity 5 or 6. Dull dark blue dome, glossy light-blue legs.
- Unitoy M5 (VV F5). Same continuous Hong Kong line. Loose dome, gaps underneath, 41-back, small red eye or a double-painted eye. Not the early halftone Unitoy.
- Taiwan M6 (VV F6). 12-back only. Large orange-red eye. Halftone wedge.
- Kader M7 (VV F7). Cavity 3 or 4. Same paint as Kader M3, made alongside it.

## Not part of the core Kenner carded line

Takara wind-up. Family 1-1 is a JAPAN COO with no Takara stamp, smaller dome rim and larger chrome buttons. Family 1-2 adds Takara on the back of the torso. Family 2 is No COO and no Takara stamp, with a larger dome rim and smaller chrome buttons.

Droid Factory. Unique sculpt with a removable third leg. Kenner stickers have a wavy or straight top line. The Palitoy sticker is very wavy. The artwork is reversed.

## Myths

There is no golden dome. That is aged lacquer. There is no dimple dome. That is a short shot. There is no No-COO solid-dome Kenner R2. A No-COO body on a solid dome is a swapped Sensorscope body. The 1978 No COO Retorno is a real Lili Ledy figure, not that myth.
