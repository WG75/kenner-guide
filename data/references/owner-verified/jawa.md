This file is the durable record for the vintage Kenner Jawa (1978, 38270). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

The first question is cloth cape or no cape. A vinyl cape is extremely rare, about 0.1%. Do not lead with vinyl versus cloth.

## Where the stamp is

The stamp is on the left leg. Not the lower robe. That is an owner override of the sheet.

Every stamp is two lines, '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' and 'HONG KONG', except Kader China. Not 'Made in China'.

Family I, Kader Hong Kong: the same two lines. A vinyl cape was on the 12a card. Cloth by the 12c card. Jawa Blaster M2.

Family II 1a, Kader Hong Kong: the H of HONG KONG is under the ©. Check the photo. Jawa Blaster M3.

Family II 1b, Kader Hong Kong: the H of HONG KONG starts after the ©, under the first G. Check the photo. Jawa Blaster M3.

Family II 2a, Kader China: the licensing line only, no country. The stamp sits higher on the leg. Check the photo. Jawa Blaster M2 and Cloak M2.

Family II 2b, Kader China: the licensing line only, no country. The stamp sits lower on the leg. Check the photo. Jawa Blaster M2 and Cloak M2.

Toltoys uses Kader Hong Kong tooling. The plastic is green. The stamp is the same two lines. Bluish Jawa Blaster M3 and Vinyl Cape M2.

Family III and IV, Unitoy Hong Kong: the same two lines. No separate stamp for III and IV. Jawa Blaster M1.

Lili Ledy uses Family IV tooling and has no stamp of its own. Pilot Blaster M6.

Poch uses mixed Kader and Unitoy parts and has no stamp of its own. Jawa Blaster M1.

The Glasslite Han already uses the black M2 Jawa blaster. That link stays.
