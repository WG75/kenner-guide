This file is the durable record for vintage Kenner Darth Vader (1977/78, 38230). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

## Where the stamp is

Every Darth Vader stamp is on the back of the left thigh. The text runs vertically in two side-by-side columns.

## Kader Hong Kong

On Kader M1, M2 and M3, the H of HONG starts under the ©. Warren answered just '©'. Do not invent a different letter for each family. Use COO guide 3.2 to separate families I, II and III.

## Kader China and family III No COO

A blank raised bar is Kader China, not Kader M1. Licensing-line-only is Kader family III No COO.

## Smile and Lili Ledy

Smile Vader is always HONG KONG. Smile is not a figure without the COO.

Lili Ledy on family IV has two versions. One version has No COO. The other shows clear remnants of HONG KONG. A partly visible country counts as No COO for COO grading. Record the remnants as an identifying tell for that Lili Ledy version. VV calls the remnants version Retorno and the No COO version Regreso.

## Unitoy M6 and M7

The stamp text is the same. Unitoy M6 has two dots on each foot sole, one in the middle of the sole and one on the toes. Unitoy M7 has none.

## Other families

Top Toys has no markings at all. That is not a 'No COO' line of text. There is no copyright line and no country.

Glasslite: the belt-buckle circle is not painted white.

Takara reads '© G.M.F.G.I. 1978' and 'JAPAN'. A cross-slot screw is in the back.

PBP on family VIII has the country scarred out.

Poch is family II, with a big leftover gate scar by the stamp, and cape M17. A family I Poch was not added.

Checked live 11 Oct 2026. Red Vader links follow that page.

- M5: Kader and Smile since 12C, and PBP. Linked to Kader M1, Kader M3, Smile M4 and PBP. Kader M2 is left on M1 only. A flashing red M5 can come with Poch Darth Vader. That wording is not a settled card pairing. It is linked on the Kader M5 record only.

- M7: Darth Vader marked Taiwan. Linked to Taiwan M10 and Taiwan M11.
- M8: Glasslite Vader. The Luke Glasslite link is left as it was.
- M9: Lili Ledy Regreso Vader, and the Lili Ledy Ben. Warren confirmed the M9 sabre is the Lili Ledy Ben's. It is linked to that Ben as well as the Lili Ledy No COO Vader.
