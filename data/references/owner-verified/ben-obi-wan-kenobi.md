This file is the durable record for the vintage Kenner Ben (Obi-Wan) Kenobi (1978, 38250). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

Figure family numbers, cape mould numbers and sabre mould numbers are different series. Family II is not cape M2, and it is not sabre M2.

## Where the stamp is

The stamp is on the back of the legs. Not every Ben stamp is two lines.

Two lines, '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' and 'MADE IN HONG KONG': Unitoy family I, and Unitoy family II 1a and 1b.

Two lines, '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' and 'HONG KONG': Kader family III 1a and 1b. Poch uses that same Hong Kong stamp.

One line, the licensing line only: Lili Ledy (family II) and Kader family III 2 (No COO). Unitoy family II 2a is the same one line. Those three are the No COO Bens.

## Unitoy family I and family II

On both of the two-line Unitoy stamps, the MADE IN HONG KONG column runs past the end of 1977. That is not a tell between family I and family II. Use Variant Villain's other tells. Family I is the white-hair Unitoy. Family II with the country is the grey-hair Unitoy.

Family II 2a is Unitoy with no country. Family II 2b is Lili Ledy. Both are the licensing line only. Lili Ledy has three head sizes. Calling them Kader-style means the stamp is one line, like the Kader No COO. They are not Kader figures.

## Kader family III and Poch

Family III 1a: HONG KONG starts under the ©.

Family III 1b: HONG KONG starts under the first G of G.M.F.G.I.

Poch uses the Kader Hong Kong stamp. The black paint is glossier and thinner, and the hands are pale.

Family III 2a and 2b are Kader with no country. The licensing line is the only marking.

## No COO

Unitoy family II, Lili Ledy and Kader family III No COO share the one licensing line. The identify flow shows the Variant Villain comparison photos, not a stamp rule, and the collector matches the figure to the photo.

## Telescoping Lightsaber

Checked against the Variant Villain sabre links, 17 Oct 2026.

- M1 lettered, then M5: Kader. The No COO white-hair Trilogo Ben also uses M6.
- M1, then M2, then M3: Unitoy.
- M9: Lili Ledy. The Vader record already links this sabre to the Lili Ledy Ben. That link stays.
- The blue double-telescoping sabre is extremely rare. It is not given a new carded link. The existing double-telescoping record stays as it is.

Poch is not named on these sabre links, so no Poch Ben is given a sabre link.

## Cape

Checked against the Variant Villain cape guide, 17 Oct 2026. Cape mould numbers are not the figure family numbers.

- M5 and M6: Unitoy, family I.
- M7: Unitoy, family II.
- M8: Unitoy, family II No COO.
- M1, M2, M3 and M4: Kader. M4 is also the Kader China cape. No separate Kader China Ben stamp was named, so the China cape record is not given a figure link.
- M9: Lili Ledy.
