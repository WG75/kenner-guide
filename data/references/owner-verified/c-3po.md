This file is the durable record for vintage Kenner C-3PO (1977/78, 38220). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

## Where the stamp is

Every C-3PO stamp is on the back of the left thigh. It is not on the shin and not near the foot. The text runs vertically in side-by-side columns.

## Columns

Only M1 (Kader) and M2 (Unitoy / Lili Ledy Retorno) are three side-by-side columns: '© G.M.F.G.I.', '1977', and 'HONG KONG'.

On M1, '1977' starts on the second column just to the right of the © on the top column.

On M2, '1977' is level with the ©.

M3 (Kader / Lili Ledy Regreso), M4 (Unitoy), M5 (Kader) and M7 (Unitoy) are two side-by-side columns: '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' and 'HONG KONG'. Do not describe that as one line followed by the next.

M3 is fairly similar to the other two-column stamps. Do not invent a letter tell for it. Send the collector to COO guide 3.1.

M5: the H of HONG starts under the middle of the ©.

M4 and M7 are on the back of the left thigh. Their alignment is similar to M3. Use the guide image to separate them. Do not place the stamp near the foot.

## Licensing

Every family reads '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' except Takara M6. Takara reads '© G.M.F.G.I. 1978' with JAPAN beside it. On cavity 1b, JAPAN is reversed.

## Lili Ledy

Lili Ledy tells belong only on Lili Ledy. A dent or a hole beside the stamp. Finishes are dull gold, shiny gold, or silver. The seat is bronze-sprayed. A fat head is a C-3PO (Removable Limbs) head mixed onto this body.

These back marks are not general stamp options. Keep them off the shared stamp list.

## Factories that are not on this figure

There is no PBP C-3PO. PBP is Spain. Lili Ledy is Mexico. No C-3PO is stamped Mexico.
