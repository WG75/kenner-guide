This file is the durable record for the vintage Kenner Han Solo (1978, 38260). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

The Variant Villain page has the COO sheet and no written tells. The Poch section says content is not available yet. No Poch Han is stored.

Figure family numbers and blaster mould numbers are different series. Family II is not Rebel Blaster M2. The old figure names Unitoy M1, Kader M2, Glasslite M2 and PBP M3 are retired. Those M numbers are accessory moulds.

## Where the stamp is

The stamp is on the left leg, under the licensing details. Warren writes the licensing line as '© GMFGI 1977'.

Family I, Unitoy: two lines, '© GMFGI 1977' and 'HONG KONG'. Not 'MADE IN'.

Family II 1a, Kader: the H of HONG KONG is under the ©.

Family II 1b, Kader: the H of HONG KONG starts after the ©.

Family II 2a, Glasslite: the licensing line only.

Family II 2b, Kader with no country: the licensing line only.

Family III, PBP and Euro Kenner: the licensing line only. There is no known tell between them. Both can have different meltmarks, and that is not a tell. The result is one answer, PBP or Euro Kenner.

## Rebel Blaster

Checked against the Variant Villain blaster guide, 17 Oct 2026. Links are by factory. Early then later means both moulds stay linked.

- Unitoy: M3, then M2. Linked to family I.
- Kader: M4, then M5. Linked to family II 1a, family II 1b, and the Kader with no country.
- PBP: M5. Linked to the family III result. Euro Kenner is not named on its own on the blaster guide.
- Glasslite Han uses the black M2 Jawa blaster. That is owner-verified. It is not a Rebel Blaster.

Poch is not named. No Poch Han is given a blaster link.
