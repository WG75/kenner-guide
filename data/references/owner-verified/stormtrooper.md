This file is the durable record for the vintage Kenner Imperial Stormtrooper (1977/78, 38240). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade. No Stormtrooper is stamped Mexico.

Figure family numbers and Imperial Blaster mould numbers are different series. Kader M7 on the figure is family VII. Imperial Blaster M7 is the China-plant blaster.

## Where the stamp is

Every Stormtrooper stamp is on the back of the left thigh. Warren calls the layouts 3-line and 2-line. The app stores them as three side-by-side columns or two side-by-side columns.

Three side-by-side columns: Kader M1, Kader M7, Poch M1, Kader China and Smile M4 with the COO. Two side-by-side columns: Unitoy, and Smile M4 without the COO.

## Kader Hong Kong

On Kader M1 and Kader M7, the H of HONG starts under the ©. Do not invent a different letter for family VII, for Unitoy, or for Smile.

COO guide 3.1 family I is Kader M1. Family VII is Kader M7. Both are three side-by-side columns of HONG KONG. Separate them with the guide.

Warren also named a 3-line Kader M8. The sheet shows only two Hong Kong Kader families, I and VII. Kader M8 is not stored as a third variant. It may be Warren's number for family VII, which is stored as Kader M7, or it may belong to another figure.

## Kader China and Takara

Kader China is just 'CHINA' on a raised bar. There is no 'MADE IN'. The old 'Made in China' entries are wrong. The merged Kader/Poch/China variant is removed.

Takara is just 'JAPAN'. There is no 'MADE IN' and no raised bar. COO guide 3.1 shows 1978 beside 'JAPAN'. Warren said to keep the licensing year on the sheet. He did not confirm 1978 in a separate sentence, so the year stays flagged as the sheet year.

## No COO

The No COO split is approved. A hard torso and a very wide mouth is Lili Ledy. A thin or small mouth is PBP. Extra mouthpiece flaps is Poch. A soft torso is Smile.

Poch is not only No COO. Poch M1 uses the family I three-column HONG KONG stamp, with extra mouthpiece flaps. Poch M2 has a HONG KONG COO on family II, the two-column Unitoy stamp, again with extra mouthpiece flaps. The No COO Poch is the family III version.

Smile M4 with the COO is three side-by-side columns, family IV. Smile M4 without the COO is two side-by-side columns, '© G.M.F.G.I.' and '1977', and no country. A hard-torso Smile without the very wide mouth is not assigned. Do not send that figure to Lili Ledy.

There are no foot-sole tells and no screw tells besides Takara's cross-slot screw.

## Other families

Top Toys has no markings at all, and no peg holes. That is not a 'No COO' line of text.

Unitoy family II and family III are both two side-by-side columns of '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' and 'HONG KONG'. No letter tell. Family II with extra mouthpiece flaps is Poch M2. Family II without flaps is Unitoy. Family III with HONG KONG is Unitoy.

## Tells to store

Family I has two torso moulds. A dimple on the chest knob tells them apart. Do not say which torso is which. Kader family I and Kader China have a rocket mouth. Poch has extra mouthpiece flaps. Unitoy family II has a line mouth.

Smile has helmet back A or B. That tells the two torso moulds apart. Smile can have a rocket mouth or a standard mouth. Do not invent which helmet is A.

Lili Ledy, PBP and the No COO Poch share the hard ABS torso on family III. The mouth separates them.

Poon is a reproduction, not a COO family and not a Kenner factory button. It is very white. The feet do not point together, and the right leg sits slightly out. A larger flat area sits on top of the torso where the arm meets. The stamp is two side-by-side columns, like family II. The Poon blaster is hard and sinks.

## Imperial Blaster guide

Checked live 11 Oct 2026.

- M1 then M2: Unitoy. Linked to Unitoy family II and Unitoy family III. The guide says M2 is later, probably from 21-backs, and does not name which COO family that is. Both families stay on both moulds.
- M3 then M4: Kader Hong Kong. M3 is the earliest, probably only 12-backs, so it is linked to Kader M1 only. M4 runs from the beginning through POTF, so it is linked to Kader M1 and Kader M7. Glasslite M4 is Droids, not this Stormtrooper. The Luke Skywalker Imperial Stormtrooper Outfit link is left as it was.
- M5: Smile for the whole Smile line, and probably PBP. Linked to Smile M4, Smile M4 without the COO, and PBP. The PBP link is the guide's 'probably'.
- M6: Taiwan. Boba Fett only. The existing Boba Fett Taiwan link stays. Not this Stormtrooper.
- M7: Kader China, and Lili Ledy (blue or solid black). The Kader record is linked to Kader China. The Lili Ledy record is linked to the Lili Ledy trooper. The heading says Kader/PBP/Lili Ledy, but the M7 text does not name PBP, so PBP is not linked to M7.
- M8: Survival Kit only. Not carded with this Stormtrooper.
- M9: Takara.
- M10: Top Toys.

Poch is not named on the blaster guide. No Poch trooper is given a blaster link.
