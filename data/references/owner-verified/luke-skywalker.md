This file is the durable record for vintage Kenner Luke Skywalker (farmboy, 1977). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

## 1. Where the COO is

Luke's COO is on the back of the left leg, under "© G.M.F.G.I. 1977".

## 2. Poch

Add Poch as a factory. Poch is the Spanish factory later renamed PBP. It made figures in Spain from older moulds received from Kader, Unitoy or Smile.

Luke Poch variants:

- Blonde Poch with the Kader COO. Pale cream hands, darker greyish blotchy trousers and boots, a higher left eyebrow, and melt marks on the back.
- Brown-hair Poch with the Unitoy COO. Dark trousers and boots, and pale cream or pinkish hands and neck.
- Mixed: a Unitoy head on Kader-COO legs.

## 3. Sabre carded-with links

- M1 single-telescoping goes with Kader, Unitoy and Smile Luke on 12A/B/C, and with Taiwan Luke on 12A only.
- M2 goes with Unitoy.
- M3 goes with Unitoy, not Smile. Brown-hair Unitoy Luke from the 21-back.
- M5 goes with Kader HK, Smile, PBP and Poch, plus the existing Kader China and Meccano links.
- M6 yellow goes with the No COO Smile brown-hair Luke on Trilogo cards.
- The circled double-telescoping sabre goes with the earliest Unitoy Luke.
- M7 Taiwan and M8 Glasslite stay as they are.

## 4. Lettered double-telescoping ids

Lettered sabres are always M1. Rename double-telescoping-lightsaber--kader-m2, double-telescoping-lightsaber--smile-m2 and double-telescoping-lightsaber--unitoy-m2 to M1. The circled Unitoy sabre is unlettered, so it is not M1.

## 5. One M1 single-telescoping sabre

Merge telescoping-lightsaber--kader-m1, telescoping-lightsaber--unitoy-m1, telescoping-lightsaber--smile-m1 and telescoping-lightsaber--taiwan-m1 into one M1 entry. An unknown company made it for all four Asian factories. It was packed with Kader, Unitoy and Smile figures on 12A, 12B and 12C, and with Taiwan figures on 12A. It sinks in water.

## 6. No COO follow-up

Glasslite and Meccano Lukes have a blank raised bar. A No COO Luke with no bar points to Smile. The identify flow asks this follow-up.

## Figure tells, not sabre tells

Sabre facts stay on the sabre. They are not figure mould or paint tells. That includes Unitoy "changed from lettered to flat tip transition", Unitoy "thin tip, flat transition", and Kader "DT (lettered) saber".

Meccano "double raised bar" and "scarred raised bar" are COO-stamp tells, not cardback tells.

## PBP

PBP tells: bright blonde hair, pinkish hands and neck, and a scarred Unitoy COO.

## Factories to record

Kader China (China), Meccano (France), Glasslite (Brazil), PBP (Spain), Poch (Spain), Lili Ledy (Mexico), Takara (Japan), Top Toys (Argentina), plus Kenner (United States) and Universal Manufacturers (Taiwan) where the data names them.
