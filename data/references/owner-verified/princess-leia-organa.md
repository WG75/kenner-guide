This file is the durable record for vintage Kenner Princess Leia Organa (1977, 38190). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

The Variant Villain figure page is image-only. Its text lists families I Kader/Glasslite, II Unitoy, III Kader/Glasslite, IV Smile and V Taiwan. The Poch page is not written yet, so this record does not add a Poch Leia.

## 1. Taiwan

'MADE IN TAIWAN' is on the back of the left leg, under '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977'.

## 2. Kader M1 and Kader M3

Kader M1 and Kader M3 differ by paint and sculpt, and by stamp position. Both read 'HONG KONG' on the back of the left leg under the licensing line.

Kader M1: the H of HONG starts under the first G of G.M.F.G.I., and the G of KONG sits under the 77 of 1977.

Kader M3: the H of HONG starts under the ©, and the G of KONG ends under the 9 of 1977.

No paint colours are recorded here. Do not invent them.

## 3. Smile M4

Smile M4: the H of HONG starts under the first G of the licensing line, and the word ends before the first 1 of 1977 (shorter than Kader M1). Smile can also be No COO.

## 4. Two Glasslite entries

Keep two Glasslite entries: Glasslite M1 and Glasslite M3, sharing the Kader M1 and Kader M3 moulds respectively. Use the Kader M1 and Kader M3 sculpt tells to separate them. Both are No COO. The identify flow asks which Kader sculpt the figure shares.

## 5. Glasslite accessories, and the blaster and cape guides

Glasslite (Brazil) Leia came with a silver Jawa blaster instead of a Leia blaster. Keep that pairing. Her cape is the M8 Glasslite cape, made in Brazil, white vinyl like the others. It has a very distinctive shape that curves inwards on both sides under the armholes.

There are five Leia blaster moulds. M1 is Kader. The same mould was used by Lili Ledy, and the grey Lili Ledy blaster belongs to Endor Poncho Leia. Do not pair that grey blaster with this figure. M2 and M3 are Smile. M4 is Unitoy. M5 is Taiwan.

Cape M3 is Smile, not Lili Ledy. Cape tells use the collar, the armholes, and the crosshatch.

## 6. Factory links

Checked on 11 Oct 2026 against the Variant Villain leia-blaster and princess-leia-organa-cape guides. Variant Villain agreed with these links.

- Kader: blaster M1, cape M1 and M2.
- Smile: blaster M2 and M3, cape M3.
- Unitoy: blaster M4, cape M4 and M5.
- Taiwan: blaster M5, cape M6 and M7.
- Glasslite: silver Jawa blaster, cape M8.

The Leia blaster guide assigns M1 to Kader (also used by Lili Ledy), M2 and M3 to Smile, M4 to Unitoy, and M5 to Taiwan. It lists no Glasslite mould. The cape guide assigns M1 and M2 to Kader, M3 to Smile, M4 and M5 to Unitoy, M6 and M7 to Taiwan, and M8 to Glasslite. The silver Jawa pairing is Warren's, and it does not contradict a mould assignment on the Leia blaster guide.
