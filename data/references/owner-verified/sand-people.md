This file is the durable record for the vintage Kenner Sand People, also called the Tusken Raider (Kenner 38280). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

The cape is vinyl. There is no cloth cloak. The stamp is the first check.

## Where the stamp is

The stamp is on the back of the left leg.

The common stamp is two lines, '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977' and 'HONG KONG'. The other stamp is the licensing line only, and no country.

Family I, Unitoy Hong Kong: 1a has a wider gap between the two lines. 1b is tighter. Check the photo. Gaderffii Stick M1. Cape M1 or M3.

Family II, Unitoy Hong Kong: 1a and 1b are an end of the lines check on the sheet. Gaderffii Stick M1. Cape M1, M2 or M3.

Family II 2a and 2b, Unitoy: the licensing line only, and the mould is weaker. Check 2a and 2b on the photo. Same stick and capes as family II.

Family III, Kader Hong Kong: 1a is pictured. Family III 1b has no picture. Do not invent a tell. Gaderffii Stick M2. Cape M6.

Family IV, Kader Hong Kong: check 1a and 1b on the sheet. Gaderffii Stick M2. Cape M4 or M7.

Family IV 2, Tri-logo Meccano: the licensing line only. Meccano is always hollow tubes. Gaderffii Stick M3. Cape M7b.

Family V, Kader Hong Kong: 1a and 1b differ by how high the © sits on the leg. Check the photo. Gaderffii Stick M2. Cape M5.

Hollow tubes are the late Kader. Very dark brown bandolier and gloves, and thin horns. Gaderffii Stick M3. Cape M7b. Cape M7b is the greenish late cape on the M7 record.

Poch uses mixed family II and IV parts. The bandolier is purple-brown. Gaderffii Stick M1, sometimes M2.
