# Chewbacca (1977/78, 38210) — owner-verified

Date: 2026-10-11
Source: owner Warren, with Variant Villain
Figure: https://www.variantvillain.com/characters/sw/chewbacca/
Image: COO guide 3.2 © WBobaFett / variantvillain.com

This file is the durable record for vintage Kenner Chewbacca (1977/78, 38210). Build scripts that regenerate COO or factory-match data must apply it. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO. Never use Mint as a grade.

## Where the stamp is

The stamp is on the back of the left thigh, running vertically. The licensing line is the first column. The country text is the column beside it.

## Licensing line

Kader M2.2 (VV F2.2, the POTF re-stamp), Glasslite (F2.2) and Top Toys M6 read '© L.F.L. 1977'. All others read '© G.M.F.G.I. 1977'.

Warren's wording grouped Kader M2 as L.F.L. The COO sheet shows the earlier F2.1 Hong Kong Kader still reading G.M.F.G.I. 1977. The Hong Kong text was removed and the line re-stamped on the late POTF F2.2. Apply L.F.L. to that POTF Kader, to Glasslite, and to Top Toys. Keep G.M.F.G.I. on the F2.1 Hong Kong Kader.

## Stamp positions

Unitoy M1: the M of MADE is directly under the © and the K of KONG is under the second 7 of 1977.

Kader M2.1: the H of HONG starts under the ©, to its right, and the G of KONG ends under the second 7 of 1977.

Smile M3: the H starts under the middle of the © and the G of KONG is under the second G of G.M.F.G.I.

## No COO

Ask in this order. Unpainted pouch, then Glasslite or Top Toys. Much darker plastic, then Lili Ledy. A circle under the left foot, or reddish plastic with melt marks, then PBP. Large almond eyes, then Smile.

Show COO guide 3.2 at the stamp step, before those questions. Comparing the leg with the sheet is the easier first check.

## Two Poch Chewbaccas

Poch from Unitoy parts uses the MADE IN HONG KONG line. Light yellow-brown plastic, large turquoise eyes, melt marks.

Poch from Kader parts uses the Hong Kong Kader line. Purple-brown pouch. Reddish torso. Limbs often go green.

## Figure tells and bowcasters

Use the figure tells (pupils, pouch, plastic shade, toe). Do not file a bowcaster name as a plastic tell.

Unitoy came with the Unitoy M2 bowcaster. Smile M3 (Hong Kong) came with Smile M4 through the SW and ESB cards, then Smile M3 from the 65b/c. The late No COO Smile came with Smile M3. Glasslite came with a Zuckuss-style rifle, a Smile mould, not a bowcaster.

## Green limbs

Green limbs are faded pigment, not a variant.
