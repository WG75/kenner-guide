Name: Variant and outfit counts
Aliases: most variants, most outfits, most versions, variant counts, outfit counts, character versions, how many versions
Part of the variant and outfit count summary. Counting basis and the top of the ranking are in part 1. Family and version lists continue here. A family is a group of figures made from the same mould, even if the mould was copied or the country stamp changed. The family numbers are just labels, not the order they were made. Each factory is named once, with its Variant Villain family numbers in brackets. Do not guess a count that is not in this summary. An unverified figure has no number.
