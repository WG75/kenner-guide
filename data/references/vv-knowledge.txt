- The aim of this weapons and accessories guide is to make a comprehensive guide of all weapons and accessories released within the original Star Wars line in the
- For comparison include comparable bootlegs and repros (made till today).
- also add good information on producer, factory and belonging COO-family if known.
- In short: There is the plastic injector and the cavity mould (made out of steel, brass or aluminium.
- Therefore often referred to as steel mould).
- The product made out of plastic removed from the cavity mould has different signs and parts.
- Ejector pins help to remove the plastic product from the cavity mould and have nothing in common with gate scars.
- In terms of vintage weapons (compared to COO families) it's important to know that weapons were not shot single. They came on sprue trees
- Still those can come from the same cavity mould.
- If you call a weapon a variant it's about the different mould not necessarily the position of the EPM.

- Known Vendors
- “Kader Industrial Co. Ltd.” (known: “K”/”HG”)
- Location Hong Kong and after '83 with plants in China and Macau.
- Established in Hong Kong in 1948, Kader celebrates 60 years as a leading plastics and toys manufacturer.
- In the 70s and 80s, business from foreign clients continued to expand, leading the Company to produce such famous products as the original Star Wars figurines, Teddy Ruxpin
- The Company also started its Mainland operations, opening its first factory in the border town of Shekou in 1983.
- The move allowed Kader to overcome labour supply constraints in Hong Kong, making room for further growth.
- Kenneth Ting, the Company was listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in 1985.
- homepage: http://www.kader.com.hk/
