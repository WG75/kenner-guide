A family is a group of figures made from the same mould, even if the mould was copied or the country stamp changed. The family numbers are just labels, not the order they were made. Each factory is named once, with its Variant Villain family numbers in brackets. Do not guess a count that is not in this summary. An unverified figure has no number.

Family record, not for the reply. Variant Villain's COO family is the moulds of one character that are the same mould, including an exact duplicate and a changed country stamp. Its terminology page treats a new steel mould as slightly different, and so as another family; that is the author's view. The numbers are not the order the moulds were used.
