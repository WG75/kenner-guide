Topic: Imperial Gunnery notes for accessories Variant Villain has not guided
Source: https://imperialgunnery.com/ checked 11 Oct 2026. robots.txt on that date is a comment about content signals. It has no User-agent block and no Disallow. These notes are short verified facts. They do not replace variantvillain.com.

Site: The Vintage Star Wars Weapons and Accessories Guide, est. 2009.

4-LOM Rifle, https://imperialgunnery.com/rifles.htm
Original V1 has a circle. Original V2 has no circle. A mass-produced repro is cast in black plastic and feels more brittle than the originals.

4-LOM Cloak, https://imperialgunnery.com/clothcapesskirts.htm
V2 has a less sharp neck cut than V1, closer to a V neck, and a creamy-white lining. V1 lining is beige. A repro was noted on eBay in November 2015.

4-LOM Chest Armor, https://imperialgunnery.com/other-accessories.htm
Documented as its own piece. It is not the cloak, and the Variant Villain headings used for the completeness list are the rifle and the cloak.

Zuckuss Rifle, https://imperialgunnery.com/rifles.htm
Originals V1, V2, V3 (Lili Ledy) and V4. V4 was issued with the Glasslite Chewbacca figure and is much shinier. A painted mass-produced repro is also listed.

Skiff Vibro Staff, https://imperialgunnery.com/staffs-axes.htm
Imperial Gunnery says it came with Barada, Nikto and the Yak Face Canadian Power of the Force release. An ejector-pin mark can be on either side. The Lili Ledy staff is a darker grey than the Kenner. Owner-verified, 11 Oct 2026: Nikto's completeness piece is this staff, overriding the Variant Villain Vibro Axe heading.

Skiff Vibro Axe, https://imperialgunnery.com/staffs-axes.htm
Imperial Gunnery says it came with Lando Skiff Guard, Weequay and Klaatu.

Klaatu Skiff Force Pike, https://imperialgunnery.com/staffs-axes.htm
A separate entry from the Skiff Vibro Staff. The staff figure list does not name Klaatu in the skiff outfit.

Rancor Keeper weapon, https://imperialgunnery.com/otherweapons.htm
Titled Rancor Keeper Gaderffii Stick / Vibroblade. Original V1 and a Taiwan V2. The owner identifies this weapon as a staff, not an axe.

Rancor Keeper Hood, https://imperialgunnery.com/helmets-hoods.htm
Original V1 can show an ejector-pin mark. A second version is darker, harder, and has no ejector-pin mark.

Chief Chirpa Staff, https://imperialgunnery.com/staffs-axes.htm
Brown originals, a Lili Ledy staff with a moulded letter L, and a Top Toys staff.

Logray Staff, https://imperialgunnery.com/staffs-axes.htm
V1 has three ejector-pin marks. V3 has two, in dark brown and red brown. V4 has none. V2 is the Top Toys sculpt.

Lumat and Warok bows, https://imperialgunnery.com/otherweapons.htm
Lumat bow is light brown. Warok bow is dark brown.

Lumat Hood and Warok Hood, https://imperialgunnery.com/helmets-hoods.htm
Both are on the helmets and hoods page.

Lumat Quiver and Warok Quiver, https://imperialgunnery.com/other-accessories.htm
Both are named in the contents of the other-accessories page.

Lando Calrissian (General) cloth cape, https://imperialgunnery.com/clothcapesskirts.htm
A general-pilot cape, separate from the vinyl Bespin Lando cape.

Squid Head cloak, https://imperialgunnery.com/clothcapesskirts.htm
Squid Head Cloak and Skirt, including a Lili Ledy burgundy cloak.

Yoda cloth cloak, https://imperialgunnery.com/clothcapesskirts.htm
Yoda Original Cloth Cloak, plus three listed repros.

Prune Face cloth cape, https://imperialgunnery.com/clothcapesskirts.htm
Prune Face Original Cloth Cape, plus two listed repros.

Princess Leia poncho helmet, https://imperialgunnery.com/helmets-hoods.htm
Numbers inside include 1 with a dot, 2, 3 and 4. An ejector-pin mark is also recorded.

Princess Leia poncho belt, https://imperialgunnery.com/belts.htm
Two notches, and shorter than the Luke poncho belt. Numbers 6, 7 and 8 are recorded inside the holster.

Luke Skywalker battle poncho, https://imperialgunnery.com/clothcapesskirts.htm
Original battle poncho, plus repro V1 and repro V2. The helmets page does not list a Luke battle-poncho helmet.

Luke Skywalker poncho belt, https://imperialgunnery.com/belts.htm
Three notches, and longer than the Leia poncho belt.

Luke Stormtrooper helmet, https://imperialgunnery.com/helmets-hoods.htm
Luke Stormtrooper Helmet original, plus two listed repros.

Not found on these Imperial Gunnery pages:
The sensorscope is not a loose weapon there. It is built into R2-D2 (Sensorscope). No Luke battle-poncho helmet section was on the helmets page.
