- List of Released Vehicles
- A-Wing Fighter
- ATL Interceptor
- Side Gunner
- Originally released in 1985 and later in 1988 by Glasslite, The Droids action figure range was inspired by the animated cartoon series on the same name.
- The line released by both Kenner & Glasslite saw only one wave of action figures.
- A second wave was planned for release in 1986 by Kenner and although prototype figures exist they never saw production as the cartoon series was pulled due to
- The Droids action figure line was manufactured worldwide in no less then 5 different countries, a Production Matrix can be viewed here.
- The production matrix includes all known carded variants to date. (Aug 2013)
