Collector glossary

==============================
CORE COLLECTOR TERMS
==============================

MOC = Mint on Card  
Figure is still sealed on its original card.

OG = Original  
Can mean:
- Not a reproduction or bootleg  
- From the original vintage run  
- The first version of a figure before later variations  
Example: Luke Skywalker (original) vs later variants like Bespin, Hoth, Jedi, etc.

Mint / Minty = Like new condition  
Often overused in listings. “Minty” is informal slang.

Beater = Heavily worn loose figure  
Typically has paint loss, discolouration, loose limbs, or damage. Usually low value unless rare.

Loose = Not carded  
May or may not include accessories. Can also refer to loose limbs.

Rubs = Paint rubs, scuffs or marks on a figure.

Melt Marks = Production flaw or damage marks  
Common on Spanish Poch / PBP figures, but can appear on others. Can also be caused by play wear.

Pews = Slang for blasters / guns  
Used online to avoid moderation filters on words like gun or rifle.

==============================
PROMOTION / PACKAGING TERMS
==============================

Mail Aways = Promotional items obtained by sending off proof of purchase
Early Bird Certificate Package is a separate promotion - see references/early-bird-certificate-package.txt
Early Bird factory (Luke, Leia, R2-D2 and Chewbacca) = probable. Unitoy and Kader versions are both probable in Early Bird sets (Variant Villain: Early Bird sets were sourced from several factories); no single Early Bird factory is established. Factory codes on cards only start at the 32B backs, so a loose figure's factory is judged from sculpt and paint. Working assumption, evidence probable: Early Bird figures are Unitoy or Kader only. No Taiwan Early Bird.

DT (Double Telescoping) = Double-telescoping lightsaber
Documented for Luke Skywalker (yellow), Ben Kenobi (blue) and Darth Vader (red) only, on the earliest releases, before the standard telescoping lightsaber replaced them. Ben and Vader DT lightsabers are extremely rare. Luke is the common one, and most, but not all, Early Bird Lukes had it. Evidence: documented. See accessories/double-telescoping-lightsaber.txt.

POP (Proof of Purchase) = Logos printed on cardbacks/boxes  
Used for promotions where collectors cut them out and mailed them to Kenner or Palitoy.  
Items were typically returned in plain packaging (box with sealed bag), not retail packaging.


Early Bird accessories: the four figures came bagged in a plain white mailer box with a tray. Luke had a yellow lightsaber, usually double-telescoping (most, not all). Leia had a Leia blaster, plus a vinyl cape per several sources. Chewbacca had a bowcaster, primarily green (bowcaster colour conflict stays unresolved). R2-D2 is the only Early Bird figure with no accessory. The plain white mailer is the package, not evidence of no accessories. See references/early-bird-certificate-package.txt.

Last 17 = the final 17 Kenner Power of the Force figures, 1985.
Members, collector-standard list: 1 A-Wing Pilot; 2 Amanaman; 3 Anakin Skywalker; 4 Barada; 5 EV-9D9; 6 Han Solo (Carbonite); 7 Imperial Dignitary; 8 Imperial Gunner; 9 Lando Calrissian (General Pilot); 10 Luke Skywalker (Battle Poncho); 11 Luke Skywalker (Imperial Stormtrooper outfit); 12 Lumat; 13 Paploo; 14 R2-D2 (with pop-up lightsaber); 15 Romba; 16 Warok; 17 Yak Face.
Not members of the Last 17: Ewok Warrior, Teebo, Nien Nunb, Luke Skywalker (Jedi Knight), and Leia Organa (Boushh Disguise). B-Wing Pilot and General Madine are not Last 17 figures either.
Lumat and Paploo were originally 1984 Return of the Jedi releases (79-back), re-released in the POTF line. Yak Face is on the list and is not a US Kenner release, per Mr Vintage Star Wars. Naming differences are cosmetic (conflicting sources on the name only): Endor Outfit or Endor Poncho vs Battle Poncho; General vs General Pilot.
