- R1: — The R1 skirt is die-cut and has die-cut steps left and right on the sides (bottom). The outer shape to the top is very unique and therefore an esay spot. The material is way more rigid then normal capes. There is no crosshatch pattern present.
- R2: — The R2 skirt is die-cut and has edged curves to the bottom. The asymmetrical holes are always in the same place. There is no crosshatch pattern present.
- R3: — The R3 skirt is another one which needs further investigation. It has die-cut steps left and right and a perfect cut to the top. Yet the bottom corners have steps to it that seems suspicious. The shape is unique and therfore could be an unknown variant, yet there is no
- R4: — The R4 skirt is mainly a reproduction made for the Turkish bootleg Blue Star figure. It is die-cut professionally and probably existing in white as well.
- R5: — The R5 skirt is made of a thin flimsy material which is more translucent compared to production capes. The edges are handcut (edged curves) and therefore it is an easy spot. There is no crosshatch pattern present.
- R6: — The R6 skirt is made of a thin flimsy material which is more translucent compared to production capes. A crosshatch pattern is present on those but it is different compared to original skirts.
- R7: — The R7 skirt is made out of a rubbery material which is also kind of elastic. A crosshatch pattern is not present on those.
- R8: — The R8 skirt is handcut and only has slits to the hangers. There is no crosshatch pattern present.
- R9: — The R9 skirt is made out of a thin and flimsy material. It is handcut and only has slits to the hangers. There is no crosshatch pattern present.
