Overview:
Emperor’s Royal Guard Pike is documented in the Variant Villain accessory guide. Colour lists, mould lists and figure pairings that follow a colon or a heading are kept in full. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where the page states them):

    - – A tapered grip on the top staff handle

    - The Taiwan staff is most identifiable by the top handle, which has an off-centred “bulge”
