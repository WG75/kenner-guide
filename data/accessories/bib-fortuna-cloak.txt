Accessory Name: Bib Fortuna Cloak
Aliases: Bib Fortuna Cloak
Category: Accessory

Overview:
Bib Fortuna Cloak is documented in the Variant Villain accessory guide. Colour lists, mould lists and figure pairings that follow a colon or a heading are kept in full. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- Families and notes named on the page index
  - MI: Smile
  - M2: Kader
  - M3: Lili Ledy
  - M4: Universal Manufacturers
  - The Bib Fortuna Cloak was released with the following combinations of colour to figure:
    - Grey Cloak:
      - Bib Fortuna
      - Bib Fortuna (Lili Ledy)
    - Burgundy Cloak: unknown (the page does not list them in text).
  - There are 4 main fabrics used in different shades to it or to the lining on the Bib Fortuna Cloak. Mainly 2 weave techniques were used: twill and plain weave.
- SMILE
  - M1 was produced by SMILE in Hong Kong. The following lining colours are known:
    - reddish olive
    - olive
    - yellowish olive
- KADER
  - M2 was produced by KADER in Hong Kong. The following lining colours are known:
    - brownish grey
- LILI LEDY
  - M3 was produced by LILI LEDY in Mexico.
  - The following lining colours are known:
    - burgundy (not pictured)
    - dark white
    - bright white
    - The following fabric colours are known:
      - grey
      - greenish grey
      - brown (probably discoloured)
- UNIVERSAL MANUFACTURERS
  - M4 was produced by UNIVERSAL MANUFACTURERS  in TAIWAN .
    - The following combinations of lining to fabric colours are known:
      - blueish green / dark grey
      - green / grey
      - olive / brown
