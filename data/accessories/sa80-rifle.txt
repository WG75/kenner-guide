Accessory Name: SA80 Rifle
Aliases: SA80 Rifle
Category: Accessory

Overview:
SA80 Rifle is documented in the Variant Villain accessory guide. Colour lists, mould lists and figure pairings that follow a colon or a heading are kept in full. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- Families and notes named on the page index
  - MI: Palitoy
  - The SA80 rifle is originally from the Kenner G.I. Joe Action Force line and was also released with the Imperial Stormtrooper in Hoth Battle Gear in the YPS comic (#510) as gimmick. It seems like they ran out of Imperial Hoth rifles and paired some of the Snowtrooper figures with the next best thing: SA80 rifles from the G.I Joe line.
  - SA80 Rifle:
    - Imperial Stormtrooper (Hoth Battle Gear)
  - There are 4 main colour variations. Only the black one was released (partially) with the Imperial Stormtrooper in Hoth Battle Gear on the YPS comic.
- M1: PALITOY
  - The SA80 was produced by PALITOY in the United Kingdom. The following colours are known:
    - Black (only one that appeared with Hoth Stormtrooper on the YPS comic)
    - Silver
    - White
    - Red
    - BLACK

Identification: use the mould and colour notes above. A COO stamp alone does not prove the accessory mould.
Rarity: unknown unless a section above states it.
