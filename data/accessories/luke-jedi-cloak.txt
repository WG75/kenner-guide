  - Made of a more rigid material, wider hood, sloppy stitching, and neatly cut. Note both female and male stud are stitched unlike any original stud. Bottom of the cape is also cut straight unlike any original. Snap stitch is very visible on the exterior. Just one of those accessories that you will see floating about. They do seem to sell quick on eBay. Thank you to the donator.
