Accessory Name: Vibro Axe
Aliases: Vibro Axe
Category: Accessory

Overview:
Vibro Axe is documented in the Variant Villain accessory guide. Colour lists, mould lists and figure pairings that follow a colon or a heading are kept in full. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- Families and notes named on the page index
  - MOULD COMPARISON
  - M1:  Unitoy
  - M2: Smile
  - M3: Lili Ledy
  - M1: Unitoy
  - Lighter grey plastic
  - Deep EPM on the grip halfway up the handle
  - Mid grey plastic
  - There’s two alternating versions of this axe, with the EPMs found mirrored on either side of the axe. These were shot in different cavities of the same steel tooling.
  - A unique mould, roughly based on the Smile HK factory sculpt.
  - Found commonly lettered “A” or “B”, but can also be found with a blank area instead of a letter.
  - Notably thicker axe blade than the Kenner version.
  - Found commonly in a range of darker grey colours. A distinctly lighter grey version was also produced.
