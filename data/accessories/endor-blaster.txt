Accessory Name: Endor Blaster
Aliases: Endor Blaster
Category: Accessory

Overview:
Endor Blaster is documented in the Variant Villain accessory guide. Colour lists, mould lists and figure pairings that follow a colon or a heading are kept in full. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- Families and notes named on the page index
  - MOULD COMPARISON
  - M1: Unitoy
  - M2: Universal Manufacturers
  - M3: Smile
  - M4: Kader
  - The Endor Blaster was usually released with the following combinations of colour to figure:
    - Grey/Grey-Blue Endor Blaster:
      - AT-ST Driver
      - Princess Leia Organa (Combat Poncho)
      - B-Wing Pilot
    - Black Endor Blaster:
      - A-Wing Pilot
      - Imperial Gunner
  - There are 4 main mould variations. Subvariants do exist and may not all be documented. As a result of the fact that these were shot on a sprue tree in higher numbers the subvariants may vary heavily depending on the steel mould used.
- UNITOY
  - M1 was produced by UNITOY in Hong Kong and can come in two main colors:
    - bluesih grey
    - grey
- UNIVERSAL MANUFACTURERS
  - M2 was produced by UNIVERSAL MANUFACTURERS in TAIWAN and can come in three main colors:
    - dark grey
    - very dark grey
    - very dark bluesih grey
- SMILE
  - M3 was produced by SMILE in Hong Kong and can come in two main colors:
    - blue
    - black
- KADER
  - M4 was produced by KADER in Hong Kong and can come in three main colors:
    - grey-blue
    - blue-grey
    - black

Identification: use the mould and colour notes above. A COO stamp alone does not prove the accessory mould.
Rarity: unknown unless a section above states it.
