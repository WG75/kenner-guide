Accessory Name: Pop-Up Saber
Aliases: Pop-Up Saber
Category: Accessory

Overview:
Pop-Up Saber is documented in the Variant Villain accessory guide. Notes below are only what that page states. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- MOULD COMPARISON: M1 & M2
- COLOUR COMPARISON: M1 & M2 — Above you can see a colour comparison between all colours known from M1 and M2 produced by SMILE and GLASSLITE . The colour from M1 is closest to the GLASSLITE version, but a bit darker and only semi translucent.
- Smile — M1 was produced by SMILE in Hong Kong. It was used for R2-D2 pop-up saber figure (POTF). It floats when submerged in water
- Smile/ Glasslite — M2 was produced by SMILE in Hong Kong. It was used for R2-D2 pop-up saber figure (POTF, Trilogo) and the DROIDS R2-D2. The molds were also used by GLASSLITE in Brazil for POTF and DROIDS R2-D2. The following colours are known:
- GW ACRYLIC R2-D2 POP UP SABER DISPLAY CASE — Easy to use, the R2-D2 Pop-up Lightsaber accessory holder is supplied in 2 parts – BASE and TOP PLATE – and are specifically designed to accompany the GW Acrylic LASER cases .
- UZAY — This pop-up saber was produced by SB PRODUCTS for the “UZAY Savaşçıları” line in Turkey . The mould was only used in the late 80s and came with Artoo Detoo (R2-D2):
- R1: Painted Saber — The R1 is one of the most common reproductions out there. It has been around for decades. They are painted in different shades of green. They sink when submerged in water.
- R2: SLC — The R2 is also a very common reproduction out there. It has been pushed into the market by SLC and is now literally everwhere. They came in two shades of green to mimic both colours from Smile and Glasslite. They sink when submerged in water.
- R3: Waxy Saber — The R3 is a scarce reproduction out there. The surface of those feels kind of waxy. They do mostly resemble the M2 version from GLASSLITE and they actually float when submerged in water.
