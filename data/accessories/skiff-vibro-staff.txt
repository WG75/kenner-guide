Accessory Name: Skiff Vibro Staff
Aliases: Skiff Vibro Staff; Skiff Vibro staff
Category: Accessory

Overview:
Skiff Vibro Staff is an owner-verified guide, 11 Oct 2026. Variant Villain says this staff is yet to be documented, so this file is not a Variant Villain page. It has exactly two variants. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where a cited source states them):
- Exactly two variants, owner-verified 11 Oct 2026
  - Smile M1 Skiff Vibro Staff. The Kenner/Smile staff. Barada and Yak Face were made only by Smile, so they came with this mould. A Smile Nikto came with this mould.
  - Lili Ledy M2 Skiff Vibro Staff. Imperial Gunnery notes this staff is a darker grey than the Kenner/Smile one. A Lili Ledy Nikto came with this mould. https://imperialgunnery.com/staffs-axes.htm
- Barada, https://www.variantvillain.com/characters/potf/barada/
  - Made only by Smile in Hong Kong. No country of origin stamp, date stamp only. The 1985 date can be a 5 restamped over the 4 of 1984.
  - 1984 date stamp. Dark red head scarf, dull red trousers. Sometimes packed in the second-issue Sears Jabba the Hutt Dungeon playset.
  - 1985 date stamp. Bright red head scarf, brighter red trousers. Packed in that playset, and on Trilogo and 92-back Power of the Force cards.
  - The staff with Barada is the Smile M1 Skiff Vibro Staff.
- Yak Face, https://www.variantvillain.com/characters/potf/yak-face/
  - Made only by Smile in Hong Kong. No country of origin stamp, date stamp only. Released on Trilogo and 92-back Power of the Force cards.
  - Head plastic is tanned, or lighter. The lighter head may be degradation.
  - A Trilogo Yak Face was sometimes packed with a black M1 Palace Blaster, and often with no accessory. The completeness piece is the Smile M1 Skiff Vibro Staff.
- Nikto, https://www.variantvillain.com/characters/rotj/nikto/
  - Made by Smile and by Lili Ledy. Both figure moulds are No COO, and the stamp does not separate them.
  - A Smile Nikto came with the Smile M1 Skiff Vibro Staff. A Lili Ledy Nikto came with the Lili Ledy M2 Skiff Vibro Staff.
  - Owner-verified, 11 Oct 2026. This staff overrides the Variant Villain heading, Skiff Vibro Axe.
- Klaatu in the skiff guard outfit is not this staff. Imperial Gunnery lists a separate Klaatu Skiff Force Pike. The staff figure list names Barada, Nikto and Yak Face.

Identification: use the two moulds above. Barada and Yak Face take Smile M1. Nikto takes Smile M1 or Lili Ledy M2, matching the figure's factory. A COO stamp alone does not prove the accessory mould when the figure's stamp is shared.
Rarity: unknown unless a section above states it.
