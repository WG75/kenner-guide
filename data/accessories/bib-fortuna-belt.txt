Accessory Name: Bib Fortuna Belt
Aliases: Bib Fortuna Belt
Category: Accessory

Overview:
Bib Fortuna Belt is documented in the Variant Villain accessory guide. Colour lists, mould lists and figure pairings that follow a colon or a heading are kept in full. Unstated moulds, colours and figure pairings are unknown.

Which figures / mould and colour notes (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- Families and notes named on the page index
  - MOULD COMPARISON
  - MI: Smile
  - M2: Kader/Lili Ledy
  - M3: Universal Manufacturers
  - Bib Fortuna
  - Bib Fortuna (Lili Ledy)
  - There are 3 main mould variations plus subvariants on the Bib Fortuna Belt. As a result of the fact that these were shot on a sprue tree in higher numbers each shot the subvariants may vary heavily depending on the steel mould used.
- SMILE
  - M1 was produced by SMILE in Hong Kong. The following colours are known:
    - yellowish grey
    - grey
- LILI LEDY
  - M2 was produced by KADER in Hong Kong and LILI LEDY in Mexico. The following colours are known:
    - light grey
    - yellowish light grey
    - grey
    - dark grey (Lili Ledy)
- UNIVERSAL MANUFACTURERS
  - M3 was produced by UNIVERSAL MANUFACTURERS in TAIWAN . The following colours are known:
    - grey
    - yellowish grey

Identification: use the mould and colour notes above. A COO stamp alone does not prove the accessory mould.
Rarity: unknown unless a section above states it.
