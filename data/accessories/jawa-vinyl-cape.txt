Accessory Name: Jawa Vinyl Cape

Category:
Clothing / Soft Goods

Used By:
Jawa (very early production)

Description:
The vinyl cape is the original body covering included with the earliest Jawa figures. It is made from thin brown vinyl, not fabric. It was replaced very early in production by the cloth cloak. Genuine vinyl capes are scarce because that production window was short.

Source: owner VF-CB reference pack, for the mould and construction notes below. The "replaced very early" timing is the live figure note. The pack's "later release" wording for the cloth cloak is not used.

Key Characteristics:
- Smooth plastic material
- No stitching
- Holds its shape more than fabric
- Slightly rigid compared to cloth
- Two arm holes. Spacing, alignment, size and the punch edge are the main mould tells
- Top curve, vinyl thickness, surface texture and edge cut are the other tells

Colour:
- Light brown, medium brown, and orange-brown or reddish tones are all seen
- Ageing changes the tone. Colour alone does not identify the mould

Mould notes from the owner pack:
- M1: cleaner hole punches, balanced spacing, smoother vinyl surface
- M2: hole placement and the top curve differ from M1, and the difference is visible side by side
- Further examples differ in hole spacing, cape width and edge trimming
- Collectors also group extra cutting and batch differences as R types (R1 through R11 and similar). Those are hole spacing, vinyl tone, edge trimming and surface finish, not a second accessory

Common Confusion:
The vinyl cape is not the cloth cloak. Reproductions often have the wrong hole spacing, the wrong thickness, the wrong outline, or a bright unnatural tone.
