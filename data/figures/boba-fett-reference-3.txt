COO / factory and body notes from the source page (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- TAIWAN
  - TAIWAN
  - Above you can see different Boba Fett figures from Fam IV made in Taiwan by an unknown factory. The Taiwan Boba Fett can come in six different main colours regarding the chest armour and also in six main colours regarding the belt colours. There is also an oddity here, because some came with so called “painted” helmets.
  - There is many theories why the “pianted helmet”  is produced like it is. Please also check out the Mould-Colour Guide . In fact there is three main colours on those painted Fett helmets: light blue, blue and dark blue. The three figures have their helmets additionally sprayed blue, after the brown and the red colour was applied. One explanation would be that these are from a batch of factory errors were way too much red and brown colour was applied and with the additional blue paint this was corrected.
