Overview:
Princess Leia Organa (in Combat Poncho) is a vintage Kenner Star Wars figure in the Return of the Jedi guide on Variant Villain. The notes below are only what that page states. Anything the page does not establish is unknown. Do not invent a variant.

Early Bird: not an Early Bird figure. Early Bird is Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Chewbacca and R2-D2 only.

Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. Workbook figure-level family range (not a confirmed debut): ROTJ 65/77/79-back; later Trilogo/POTF-era carding varies by figure.
