Overview:
Artoo-Detoo (R2-D2) with pop-up Lightsaber is a vintage Kenner Star Wars figure in the Power of the Force guide on Variant Villain. The notes below are only what that page states. Anything the page does not establish is unknown. Do not invent a variant.

Early Bird: not an Early Bird figure. Early Bird is Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Chewbacca and R2-D2 only.

Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. Workbook figure-level family range (not a confirmed debut): POTF 92-back and/or European Trilogo 70-back. Exact micro-variant carding requires sealed evidence. That debut line is the workbook figure entry already in the debut-cardbacks reference, not a new claim. Compatible cardbacks are separate from factory matching. Factory matching is not established unless a source states the link.

COO / factory and body notes from the source page (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- Figure Guide — R2-D2 was again updated in the late Trilogo/ Power of the Force era, to remove the Sensorscope and include a full length removable green lightsaber to recreate the scene with Luke Skywalker over the Sarlacc pit in Return of the Jedi.
- Smile/ Glasslite — This figure, and the Droids era cartoon version of R2-D2 were solely produced by the SMILE factory in Hong Kong. As the popularity of the line slowed, these late era figures had their scale of production decreased and only a single factory was required to meet the demand.
- Sticker Guide: — The only relevant picture for Pop-up R2-D2 is the F2 Smile “Late-era”. The sticker, screw & moulding guides are a general overview of all R2 figures.
- SCULPT & SCREW DETAILS: — The only relevant picture for Pop-up R2-D2 is the “Smile Pop-up”. The sticker, screw & moulding guides are a general overview of all R2 figures.
