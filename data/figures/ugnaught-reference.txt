  - The Asian factories involved are Unitoy (3L MIHK & 1L no COO), Kader (4L MIHK) & Smile (2L HK). Poch (early PBP) imported mostly unpainted Kenner parts and accessories to construct their figures for distribution across the Spanish market.
  - The Kader Ugnaught was distributed solely to the international markets. I’ve found examples of Kaders on Toltoys, Palitoy, Clipper, Kenner Canada, General Mills and Meccano cards, yet none on a standard US Kenner card.

- Point of Interest: This figure was included in the Cloud City Playset , but made its first appearance on The Empire Strikes Back 41 back card. This figure also appeared on a Canadian exclusive skin wrapped card*.
- Major variations: Though all vintage figures have minor variations, The Ugnaught figure’s only perceived major variation isn’t even on the molded figure itself, but instead on its material smock. Many collectors assume the smock was manufactured using both purple or blue cloth, but evidence is proving the color change to be a result of age and storage conditions. Their is no concrete proof that Kenner ever produced a purple smock. There is rumoured to be a green smock, but there is absolutely no photographic evidence to support the rumour. *The Canadian Skin Wrapped figures were produced for Sears Canada and were included as exclusive bonus figures in multi-packs created for the holiday catalog.
