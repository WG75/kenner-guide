Overview:
Teebo is a vintage Kenner Star Wars figure from the Return of the Jedi line. Lists under a heading or a colon are kept in full from the Variant Villain page. Anything that page does not establish is unknown. Do not invent a variant.

Early Bird: not an Early Bird figure. Early Bird is Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Chewbacca and R2-D2 only.

Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. Workbook figure-level family range (not a confirmed debut): ROTJ 65/77/79-back; later Trilogo/POTF-era carding varies by figure.
Workbook block: data/compatibility/debut-cardbacks-reference-rotj-4.txt under Figure Name: Teebo. That block is the debut record. Compatible cardbacks on the Variant Villain page are not a substitute for it. Factory matching is not established unless a source states the link.

- Notes from the page with the photo credit name removed
  - Teebo is again one of my favourites on Lili Ledy. It is, together with C-3PO removable limbs, the hardest figure to get complete (only counting the standard LL figures). I have pictured two totally different versions which can be both only found in Mexico.
  - What you can notice first is the different fur colour (lighter and darker stripes) and the slightly different colour to the “feathers” on the headpiece.
  - On the picture below you can also notice the main difference very quick: the eye colour.
  - The Teebo on the left has grey eyes and the Teebo to the right has very brown eyes. Both eye colours, as well as both fur colours, are unique compared to all “Kenner” produced figures.
