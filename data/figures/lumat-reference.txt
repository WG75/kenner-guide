Overview:
Lumat is a vintage Kenner Star Wars figure from the Return of the Jedi line. Lists under a heading or a colon are kept in full from the Variant Villain page. Anything that page does not establish is unknown. Do not invent a variant.

Early Bird: not an Early Bird figure. Early Bird is Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Chewbacca and R2-D2 only.
Last 17: yes (Last 17). One of the final Kenner Power of the Force figures, 1985. See term_last_17 in data/terms/collector_terms.json. Lumat was originally a 1984 Return of the Jedi release (79-back) and was re-released in the POTF line.

- Lili Ledy
  - Above you can see following variations (left to right):
    - Dark brown eyes, mid grey-brown hood
    - Very dark brown eyes, light grey-brown hood
  - LILI LEDY in Mexico produced the Lumat figure and accessories. In a continual search for cheaper bases of production, Kenner added Lili Ledy to their rota of vendors in Asia, to include their factory over the US border in Tijuana, Mexico. With greater Kenner oversight, the quality control in this Tijuana plant was higher than the previous output from Lili Ledy’s Mexico City operation.
  - The tooling for Lumat was only used in Mexico, so only minor paint variations exist on this figure. No country of origin or date stamps were ever applied.
  - Lumat was released on Lili Ledy 50bk [50-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review], ROTJ 79, Trilogo and 92bk POTF cardbacks.
  - Lumat was packaged with his own unique hood, quiver and light brown bow. These accessories are yet to be documented on Variant Villain.
