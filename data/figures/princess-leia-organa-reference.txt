Figure Name: Princess Leia Organa

Character Overview:
Princess Leia Organa is a central character in the Star Wars franchise. Introduced in Star Wars (1977), she is a princess of Alderaan, a member of the Imperial Senate, and a leader in the Rebel Alliance. She plays a key role in the destruction of the Death Star and later helps defeat the Empire. Portrayed by Carrie Fisher.

Collector Names / Aliases:
Leia, White Leia, The Princess, Organa, OG, O.G., Original, First 12, 12 Back Leia

Release Information:
- Released: 1978–1984
- Appears on: Star Wars, Empire Strikes Back, Return of the Jedi cardbacks
- Early Bird Certificate Package figure (see references/early-bird-certificate-package.txt). Working assumption, evidence probable: Early Bird figures are Unitoy or Kader only. No Taiwan Early Bird.
- Early Bird accessory: Leia blaster, plus a vinyl cape per several sources.
- Part of original Wave A 12 Back figures

LEIA BLASTER

General:
- Typically black-blue in colour
- Exclusive to Princess Leia Organa in the original line

Mould Variants:

M1 – Kader / Lili Ledy  
M2 – Smile  
M3 – Smile  
M4 – Unitoy  
M5 – Taiwan  

Notes:
- 5 main moulds + subvariants
- The grey Lili Ledy blaster is M1, for Endor Poncho Leia. Do not pair it with this figure.
- Glasslite (Brazil) Leia came with a silver Jawa blaster instead of a Leia blaster, and with the M8 cape.
- Produced in high-volume sprue trees
- Variation influenced by mould differences and production conditions

Collector Notes:

Owner-verified COO (Warren, 11 Oct 2026):

The country stamp is on the back of the left leg, under "© G.M.F.G.I. 1977". A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO.

Kader M1 and Kader M3 both read HONG KONG. They differ by paint and sculpt, and by where the stamp sits. On Kader M1 the H of HONG starts under the first G of G.M.F.G.I., and the G of KONG sits under the 77 of 1977. On Kader M3 the H of HONG starts under the ©, and the G of KONG ends under the 9 of 1977.

Smile M4 reads HONG KONG with the H of HONG under the first G of the licensing line, and the word ends before the first 1 of 1977. That stamp is shorter than Kader M1. Smile can also be No COO.

'MADE IN TAIWAN' is on the back of the left leg under the licensing line.

Glasslite M1 and Glasslite M3 are both No COO. They share the Kader M1 and Kader M3 moulds respectively. Glasslite (Brazil) came with a silver Jawa blaster instead of a Leia blaster, and with the M8 cape. The M8 cape is white vinyl and curves inwards on both sides under the armholes.

The grey Lili Ledy blaster is M1 for Endor Poncho Leia. Do not pair it with this figure. Cape M3 is Smile, not Lili Ledy.
