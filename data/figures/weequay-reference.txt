Figure Name: Weequay
Aliases: Weequay
Packaging: Return of the Jedi
Category: Figure

Overview:
Weequay is a vintage Kenner Star Wars figure from the Return of the Jedi line. Lists under a heading or a colon are kept in full from the Variant Villain page. Anything that page does not establish is unknown. Do not invent a variant.

Early Bird: not an Early Bird figure. Early Bird is Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Chewbacca and R2-D2 only.

Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. Workbook figure-level family range (not a confirmed debut): ROTJ 65-back onward; later 77/79/Trilogo carding varies by figure.
Workbook block: data/compatibility/debut-cardbacks-reference-rotj-2.txt under Figure Name: Weequay. That block is the debut record. Compatible cardbacks on the Variant Villain page are not a substitute for it. Factory matching is not established unless a source states the link.

COO / factory and body notes from the source page (evidence: documented where the page states them):
- Families and notes named on the page index
  - I: Unitoy
  - II: Smile/ Lili Ledy
  - III: Unitoy
  - DON’T RELY ON JUST THE COO TO IDENTIFY A FIGURE.  Mould, paint colour, plastic colour and figure assembly traits are also needed to confirm your figure’s origins.

- Smile
  - Left: “© LFL 83” (wide gap) Right “H.K.” or smooth NCOO

  - Tan coloured trouser plastic

  - Left: “© LFL 83” (wide gap) Right smooth NCOO
  - Turquoise arm & torso
  - Glossy darker grey braids
  - Darker tan coloured trouser plastic

  - Left: “© LFL 83” (lower C, no gap) Right “H.K.” or smooth NCOO
  - Turquoise arm & torso
  - Light or dark brown hands / NCOO Dark brown hands

Variant questions:
1. Country of origin (COO) is the small text on the back of the legs saying where the figure was made. Use only the stamps named for this figure. A partial or cut-off country name counts as No COO.
2. Which head, paint or body trait matches a section above?
3. Which accessory mould or colour is with it?
4. If carded, which cardback family (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92)? 48-back and regional numbers 14, 17, 18, 30, 37, 50 and 70 are not on that list.

Accessory factory:
- Families listed: I Unitoy, II Smile/ Lili Ledy, III Unitoy. Several factories, so the axe mould is not determinable from the figure alone.
- The contents heading "VIBRO AXE" links to the Vibro Axe page. That page's further reading links this figure guide, and it does not say which mould Weequay carries. Its moulds are M1 Unitoy, M2 Smile and M3 Lili Ledy.
