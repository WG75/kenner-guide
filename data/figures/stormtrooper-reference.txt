Figure Name: Imperial Stormtrooper

Character Overview:
The Imperial Stormtrooper is the standard infantry soldier of the Galactic Empire. First appearing in Star Wars (1977), Stormtroopers are instantly recognisable by their white armour and black detailing. They are one of the most iconic troop builders in the Star Wars line.

Collector Names / Aliases:
Stormtrooper, Stormy, Stormie, Trooper, TK, White Trooper

Release Information:
- Released: 1978–1985
- Appears on: Star Wars, Empire Strikes Back, Return of the Jedi cardbacks
- Part of original Wave A 12 Back figures

--------------------------------------------------

ACCESSORIES

Primary Accessory:
- Imperial Blaster (E-11 style)

Accessory Matching Rules:
- Multiple moulds and colour variations exist
- Correct pairing depends on COO family and production period
- Extremely high variation due to mass production

--------------------------------------------------

IMPERIAL BLASTER (SUMMARY)

General:
- One of the most widely produced accessories in the entire line
- Used across multiple figures (not Stormtrooper-exclusive)

Key Notes:
- Large number of moulds (M1–Mx variants)
- Colour ranges:
  - black
  - dark blue
  - mid blue
  - light blue / grey-blue

Important:
- Blaster ID should be handled separately in a dedicated accessory file

--------------------------------------------------

STAMP

The stamp is on the back of the left thigh. Three side-by-side columns: Kader M1, Kader M7, Poch M1, Kader China and Smile with the COO. On Kader M1 and Kader M7, the H of HONG starts under the ©. Kader China is 'CHINA' on a raised bar, not 'Made in China'. Unitoy is two side-by-side columns. Smile without the COO is two columns and no country. Top Toys has no markings at all.

FIGURE VARIANT FAMILIES

- Family I: Kader M1, Poch M1 and Kader China. Rocket mouth. A dimple on the chest knob tells the two torso moulds apart. Poch M1 has extra mouthpiece flaps.
- Family II: Unitoy, and Poch M2. Poch M2 has a HONG KONG COO. Line mouth. Extra mouthpiece flaps mark Poch.
- Family III with HONG KONG is Unitoy. With no country, a hard torso and a very wide mouth is Lili Ledy, a thin or small mouth is PBP, and extra mouthpiece flaps is Poch.
- Family IV: Smile. With the COO it is three side-by-side columns. Without the COO it is two columns and a soft torso. Helmet back A or B tells the torso moulds apart.
- Family V: Takara. Just 'JAPAN', no 'MADE IN', and a cross-slot screw. Warren confirmed '© G.M.F.G.I. 1978' with 'JAPAN'.
- Family VI: Top Toys. No markings at all, and no peg holes.
- Family VII: Kader M7. Three side-by-side columns. The H of HONG starts under the ©. Warren's Kader M8 is this Kader M7.

--------------------------------------------------

GENERAL PRODUCTION NOTES

- Stormtrooper is one of the highest volume figures produced
- Heavy variation exists across:
  - plastic tone (bright white → cream)
  - helmet sculpt
  - torso material
  - COO markings

Important:
- COO alone is not sufficient for identification
- Must combine:
  - torso type
  - helmet details
  - plastic colour
  - mould features

--------------------------------------------------

COLOUR VARIATION

Common Tones:
- Bright white
- Off-white / cream
- Slight yellowing (age or production)

Notes:
- Discolouration vs factory tone can be difficult to separate
- Compare across limbs and torso for consistency

--------------------------------------------------

HELMET VARIATION

Look for:
- Eye paint shape
- Mouth vent paint
- Back mould line (arc vs flat)
- Symmetry differences

Important:
- Helmet sculpt is one of the key identifiers across COO families

--------------------------------------------------

REPRODUCTIONS (IMPORTANT)

Stormtrooper is one of the most reproduced figures.

Warning Signs:
- Incorrect plastic tone (too bright or too matte)
- Soft or incorrect helmet details
- Incorrect proportions
- Poor paint alignment
- Incorrect COO or missing expected traits

Collector Tip:
- Always compare:
  - helmet sculpt
  - torso feel
  - blaster style
  - COO marking

--------------------------------------------------

COMMON MISTAKES

- Confusing hard torso with Lili Ledy / PBP
- Over-relying on COO alone
- Ignoring helmet sculpt differences
- Assuming all white figures are identical
- Not checking torso material

--------------------------------------------------

BLASTERS

Imperial Blaster moulds are a different series from the figure families. Kader uses M3 then M4. Kader China uses M7. Unitoy uses M1 then M2. Smile uses M5. PBP uses M5. Lili Ledy uses M7. Takara uses M9. Top Toys uses M10. Taiwan M6 is Boba Fett only. Kader M8 is the Survival Kit only.

POON REPRODUCTION

Poon is a reproduction, not a COO family. It is very white. The feet do not point together, and the right leg sits slightly out. A larger flat area sits on top of the torso where the arm meets. The stamp is two side-by-side columns, like family II. The Poon blaster is hard and sinks.
