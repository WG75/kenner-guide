Overview:
Gamorrean Guard is a vintage Kenner Star Wars figure from the Return of the Jedi line. Lists under a heading or a colon are kept in full from the Variant Villain page. Anything that page does not establish is unknown. Do not invent a variant.

Early Bird: not an Early Bird figure. Early Bird is Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Chewbacca and R2-D2 only.

Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. Workbook figure-level family range (not a confirmed debut): ROTJ 65-back onward; later 77/79/Trilogo carding varies by figure.
Workbook block: data/compatibility/debut-cardbacks-reference-rotj-1.txt under Figure Name: Gamorrean Guard. That block is the debut record. Compatible cardbacks on the Variant Villain page are not a substitute for it. Factory matching is not established unless a source states the link.

Cardbacks named on the Variant Villain page (documented cardings, not a confirmed debut):
- Kader HK’s Gamorrean Guard is found on 65 & 77bk Kenner cards. Different combinations of dark/mid/light silver and painted/unpainted eyes will be available beyond what’s shown above.
- The Unitoy figure had a short release on the HT 65a/b cardbacks.
- Later, the moulds were moved from Unitoy in Hong Kong to Kader’s Macau factory. The H.K. COO was removed and stamped with a new “MACAU” COO where production continued and the figures were released on 65 and 77bk cards with the ME vendor code.
- The dark armoured version of the Gamorrean Guard has been found on the early 14bk [14-back: not on Warren's list of valid Kenner families (12, 20, 21, 31, 32, 41, 45, 47, 65, 77, 79, 92); regional/other family, flagged for his review] RETORNO cardback. The REGRESO and 65bk Kenner “Made in Mexico” releases featured the figure with either a mid or light silver armour.
