Aliases: Yak Face; Saelt-Marae
Packaging: Power of the Force
Category: Figure

Overview:
Yak Face is a vintage Kenner Star Wars figure from the Power of the Force line. Lists under a heading or a colon are kept in full from the Variant Villain page. Anything that page does not establish is unknown. Do not invent a variant.

Early Bird: not an Early Bird figure. Early Bird is Luke Skywalker, Princess Leia Organa, Chewbacca and R2-D2 only.
Last 17: yes (Last 17). One of the final Kenner Power of the Force figures, 1985. See term_last_17 in data/terms/collector_terms.json. Yak Face is on this list. He is not a US Kenner release, per Mr Vintage Star Wars.

Debut Kenner Cardback: not confirmed for the figure as a whole. No figure-level family range is stated. Variant lines below are the workbook's Earliest / exact cardback values, not a single debut for every variant.
Workbook block: data/compatibility/debut-cardbacks-reference-potf-3.txt under Figure Name: Yak Face. That block is the debut record. Compatible cardbacks on the Variant Villain page are not a substitute for it. Factory matching is not established unless a source states the link.

Cardbacks named on the Variant Villain page (documented cardings, not a confirmed debut):
- SMILE in Hong Kong produced the Yak Face figure and accessories. Demand had slowed by time of the POTF line, and as a result these figures were produced in much lower numbers.  The production version is only available with a date, but no country of origin stamp.
- Yak Face was released on Trilogo and 92bk POTF cards.
- Yak Face was packaged with the Skiff Vibro staff. Occasionally the Trilogo Yak Face was packaged with a black M1 Palace blaster , and often the Trilogo Yak Face did not include an accessory at all. The Skiff Vibro staff is yet to be documented on Variant Villain.
