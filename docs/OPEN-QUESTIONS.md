# Open questions

Items that need Warren's judgement. Nothing here blocked the build. Where sources disagree, both are recorded and neither was picked.

## Early Bird factories

Variant Villain's position, already in the reference files (evidence: probable): Unitoy and Kader versions are both probable in Early Bird sets; Early Bird sets were sourced from several factories; no single Early Bird factory is established. Factory codes on cards only start at the 32B backs, so a loose figure's factory is judged from sculpt and paint.

Working assumption added everywhere those factories are discussed (evidence: probable): Early Bird figures are Unitoy or Kader only. No Taiwan Early Bird.

The "M2 Unitoy Version" lines on Early Bird Luke and Leia were removed. They now use that working assumption. An earlier "Kader M3" claim for Early Bird R2-D2 was already removed and is still unverified. The Unitoy very-dark-blue dome line in the R2 debut block is one Early Bird-era carded example, not proof that every Early Bird R2-D2 was Unitoy.

## Chewbacca bowcaster colour

Still unresolved, both wordings kept in `data/figures/chewbacca-reference.txt`:
