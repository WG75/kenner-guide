# Variant Villain gaps

Figures are the 96 entries on the Wikipedia [List of Kenner Star Wars action figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Kenner_Star_Wars_action_figures), stored in `data/kenner-debut-figures.json`. The table's year column runs 1977–1985. Twelve of those figures are dated 1977; they are the first wave of the same vintage line (the shelf years collectors call 1978–1985).

Accessories are the original pieces on the Variant Villain accessory guide, plus any bubble piece from that figure list that has no guide page. A bubble piece is not listed in (c) when it is the same accessory as one of those guides, even if the guide title does not contain the figure's name. Luke, Vader and Ben lightsabers are the telescoping guides. The Rebel Blaster is the weapon older lists called a Smuggler Blaster.

A figure page "has COO/factory/mould details" when the fetched character page names a COO family, a country stamp, or a factory guide line. A verified COO choice is stricter: the bot only offers stamp buttons when the wording is known. Thirty-one figures take that wording from the owner's stamp sheet (10 Oct 2026). The others take it from the character page, including the 1980 Lando leg notes. An accessory page has details when its article names a mould, factory, or country stamp. Menu links such as "Introduction to COOs" do not count.
